Capacitor Banks: Package, Bias and Antiresonance

Should a PMIC use a row of identical 0201s, alternating values, or three different values? Start with the rail's permitted voltage error and current demand. A capacitor count or package size is not a power-integrity specification. Prerequisites: decoupling fundamentals and return current.

Design question: what impedance does the load see, over which frequencies, bias, temperature and operating modes? Preserve the device manufacturer's validated capacitor requirements while evaluating changes to the surrounding PDN.

For a first budget, use Ztarget = ΔVallowed / ΔI. A 40 mV dynamic allowance and 4 A step suggest 10 mΩ. This is a screening envelope, not proof of time-domain compliance: the load spectrum, regulator loop, package and die capacitance still matter. Reserve separate allowances for DC regulation, distribution loss, ripple and transients.

0201 versus 0402: compare part numbers, not labels

Here 0201 and 0402 mean inch codes: approximately 0.6 × 0.3 mm and 1.0 × 0.5 mm body sizes, also called metric 0603 and 1005. Do not interchange inch and metric codes in a BOM. Smaller bodies can shorten the mounting loop and fit near balls, but usable capacitance, voltage rating, assembly yield and copper geometry must be compared together.

Why “three values” can produce a worse result

A mounted branch is modeled here as Zi = ESRi + j[ωLi − 1/(ωCeff,i)]. Parallel branches combine as Zbank = jωLshared + 1 / Σ(1/Zi). Add complex admittances first, then take the magnitude. Between different self-resonances, capacitive and inductive susceptances can cancel, creating an antiresonant impedance peak.

Bogatin, Smith and Sandler distinguish measured MLCC behavior from their simulated three-value comparisons. Their Figure 6 includes a board-mounted measurement; Figure 8 compares simulated networks. The article explains why value staggering inherited from older capacitor technology is not a universal modern MLCC strategy. The Myth of Three Capacitor Values, Figures 6–8.

Equal values can also resonate with the regulator, planes or package. Mixed technologies may supply needed bulk energy and damping. Deliberately different values can be appropriate when supported by a device reference design or a verified PDN. “All equal” is no more a universal rule than “always three.”

Moving a capacitor does not abruptly make it useless at a universal distance. Its complete supply/return path changes the impedance seen by the load. Check the mesh and frequency sampling before trusting the ratios

The study refined maximum mesh edge through 1.5, 0.75, 0.35 and 0.2 mm. The mixed case needed another run at 0.14 mm. Final maximum complex changes were 1.489%, 1.030%, 1.623% and 0.618% for the four rows above. The plotted metric is 100 × |Zcoarse − Zfine| / |Zfine| at every sample. The 2% line is this study's numerical criterion, not a device requirement or proof of asymptotic convergence.

At the 0.35 mm mixed-bank mesh, increasing field solves from 12 to 24 per decade changed complex impedance by at most 0.185%. All published sweeps contain 301 impedance samples. Low-frequency capacitance was approximately 3 µF, Re(Z) stayed positive and no solver warnings were reported. These checks do not replace measurement.

What this does not establish: no regulator control loop, package/die model, manufacturer bias curve, hardware correlation or EMC result is included. The geometry adds mounting and spreading impedance to the assumed component ESL. This experiment does not rank straight/T/Y/V routing, compare real package sizes, or test permutations of the mixed values. Those require additional controlled models.

Only shared L changes in this comparison. It shifts the bank resonance as well as increasing high-frequency impedance. For the five AC runs, the maximum relative complex-impedance difference from the independent closed-form calculation was below 10−12. 0.5 nH circuit · 0.5 nH CSV.

  1. At the default values, compare all three curves. Record the peak within the target band, not only each minimum. Export the CSV so the result is reproducible.
  2. Change shared L from 0 to 0.5 nH. At 100 MHz that common path alone has reactance 0.314 Ω. Explain why adding capacitors behind it cannot divide that inductance by the capacitor count.
  3. Try 50% capacitance retention, then change ESR from 10 to 30 mΩ. Increased loss can reduce an antiresonance while worsening the minimum impedance. Decide against the entire target band.
  4. For a real design, obtain two actual 0201 and 0402 candidate models at the rail bias. Compare equal effective C, placement area and allowable height. Add extracted mounting paths and regulator/package models.
  5. Verify impedance at the load port using a suitable calibrated, de-embedded low-impedance measurement fixture, then load-step the assembled rail at the specified edge rates. A board connector measurement is not automatically the die voltage.
Why these experiments do not rank alternating physical values

Reordering parallel branches leaves the lumped network unchanged. The field study above includes geometry, but holds the order of mixed values fixed. A physical sandwich changes distances and current paths; compare those permutations with the same parts, ports and stackup before claiming an advantage.
