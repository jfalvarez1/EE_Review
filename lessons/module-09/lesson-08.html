Analog vs Digital Implementation Trade-offs

Many signal processing functions can be implemented in either the analog or digital domain. Each approach has distinct advantages. Understanding when to use each—or a hybrid approach—is essential for optimal system design.

Analog Advantages

  • Low power: No clock, no switching losses
  • Low latency: Continuous-time processing
  • Infinite resolution: No quantization
  • High bandwidth: MHz-GHz operation

Digital Advantages

  • Precision: No drift, exact coefficients
  • Flexibility: Software-configurable
  • Noise immunity: Regenerative
  • Complexity: Enables sophisticated algorithms
