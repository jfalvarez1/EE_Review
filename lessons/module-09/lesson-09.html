Delay Lines and Phase Shifters

Precise timing adjustment is essential for clock deskewing, data alignment, and signal processing. This lesson covers analog delay lines, digital delay elements, and phase interpolators.

Delay Applications

  • Clock deskewing: Align multiple clocks
  • DDR interfaces: DQS alignment
  • Radar/lidar: Range measurement
  • Correlators: Signal matching

Delay Types

  • Fixed: PCB trace, cable
  • Programmable: Digital taps
  • Analog: VCO, LC elements
  • Interpolated: Sub-gate resolution

Delay Locked Loop (DLL)

DLL vs PLL: A DLL adjusts delay rather than frequency. It cannot multiply frequency but has lower phase noise accumulation since there's no VCO integration.
