Design Margin and Derating

Design margin is the difference between a component's rating and its actual operating conditions. Proper derating extends product life and improves reliability, but excessive margin increases cost and size. This lesson covers how to establish appropriate margins for different applications and component types.

Why Derate?

  • Component variation: ±20% tolerance is common
  • Temperature effects: Ratings change with temp
  • Aging: Parameters drift over life
  • Transients: Brief excursions above nominal

Margin Considerations

  • Cost: Larger parts cost more
  • Size: Bigger physical footprint
  • Availability: Higher ratings may be rare
  • Performance: Some parameters degrade
