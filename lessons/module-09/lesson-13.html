Thermal Management Trade-offs

Heat is the enemy of electronics reliability. Every 10°C rise in junction temperature roughly halves semiconductor lifetime. However, thermal management solutions add cost, weight, and complexity. Understanding thermal trade-offs is essential for achieving both performance and reliability targets.

Heat Sources

  • Conduction losses: I²R in on-state
  • Switching losses: During transitions
  • Quiescent current: Bias and standby
  • Gate drive: Capacitive charging

Cooling Methods

  • Conduction: Heatsinks, thermal pads
  • Convection: Natural and forced air
  • Radiation: Surface finish, area
  • Phase change: Heat pipes, vapor chambers
