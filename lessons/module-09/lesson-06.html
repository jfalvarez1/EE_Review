Size vs Performance Trade-offs

Component size directly affects electrical performance. Smaller components have higher parasitics, tighter tolerances are harder to achieve, and thermal management becomes more challenging. Understanding these trade-offs is essential for modern compact designs.

Size Impacts

  • Capacitors: Smaller = higher ESR, ESL
  • Resistors: Smaller = higher noise, drift
  • Inductors: Smaller = higher DCR, lower Q
  • ICs: Smaller = worse thermal

Design Considerations

  • Power rating: Derates with size
  • Voltage rating: Limited by clearance
  • Tolerance: Harder in small packages
  • Assembly: Smaller = harder rework
