Speed vs Accuracy Trade-offs

Every analog design involves fundamental trade-offs. Higher speed typically means more power, more noise, and less precision. Understanding these relationships helps make optimal choices.

Implication
4× current = 2× less noise
Diminishing Returns: To halve the noise, you need 4× the power. Going from 10nV to 1nV requires 100× power increase. Choose your noise budget carefully!
