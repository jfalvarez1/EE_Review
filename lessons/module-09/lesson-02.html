RC Oscillators (Wien Bridge)

RC oscillators use resistors and capacitors for timing. The Wien bridge oscillator is popular for audio frequencies, providing good sine wave purity with simple component requirements.

Wien Bridge Frequency
f = 1 / (2πRC)
Required Gain
G = 3 (exactly)
Key Insight: The Wien bridge network provides 0° phase shift at f₀ with attenuation of 1/3. The amplifier must provide gain of exactly 3 for unity loop gain. Too little: no oscillation. Too much: clipping.

Wien Bridge Network Analysis

At f = f₀
Phase = 0°, Gain = 1/3
At f < f₀
Phase leads, gain < 1/3
At f > f₀
Phase lags, gain < 1/3
Practical Note: HP used a small incandescent lamp for AGC in their famous 200A audio oscillator. The lamp's thermal time constant (~100ms) provides smooth amplitude control without modulating the signal.
