Conducted Emissions

Conducted emissions are unwanted high-frequency currents that propagate along power and signal cables, coupling noise into other equipment or the power grid. Understanding the sources, measurement techniques, and mitigation strategies is essential for meeting regulatory requirements like FCC Part 15 and CISPR 22/32.

Frequency Range:150 kHz - 30 MHz
LISN Impedance:50Ω || 50μH
Limit Class A:~60 dBμV (industrial)
Limit Class B:~48 dBμV (residential)
Key Insight: Conducted emissions have two modes - differential mode (noise between Line and Neutral) and common mode (noise on both conductors relative to ground). Most emissions above 1 MHz are common mode, requiring different filtering approaches.
