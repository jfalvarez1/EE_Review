Surge Protection

Surge protection devices (SPDs) guard electronic equipment from transient overvoltages caused by lightning, load switching, or ESD. Understanding surge characteristics and protection strategies is essential for designing robust systems that survive real-world electrical disturbances.

Lightning Surge:1.2/50μs waveform
EFT Burst:5/50ns pulses
ESD:1ns rise time
Protection Goal:Clamp voltage < damage threshold
Key Insight: Effective surge protection requires multiple stages - a high-energy primary stage to absorb the bulk energy, and a fast secondary stage to clamp residual voltage to safe levels for sensitive components.
