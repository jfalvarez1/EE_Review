Load Switches & Discharge Control

Load switches control power to subsystems while managing inrush current and output discharge. Quick Output Discharge (QOD) ensures defined power-off states.

Key Insight: Without controlled discharge, output capacitors can keep a rail powered for seconds after turn-off. This prevents proper power cycling and can cause sequencing violations on restart.
