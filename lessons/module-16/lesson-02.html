ADC Fundamentals

Analog-to-Digital Converters sample analog signals and quantize them to digital codes. The choice of ADC architecture depends on speed, resolution, and power requirements.

Quantization Step
Q = Vref / 2N
Quantization Noise
SNRQ = 6.02×N + 1.76 dB
Key Insight: Quantization adds noise proportional to LSB/√12. Faster sampling doesn't improve resolution—you need more bits or oversampling with noise shaping (sigma-delta).

ADC Architectures

Architecture Speed Resolution Application
Flash 1+ GSPS 6-8 bit Oscilloscopes, video
SAR (Successive Approx) 1-10 MSPS 10-18 bit General purpose, sensors
Pipeline 10-500 MSPS 10-16 bit Communications, imaging
Sigma-Delta (ΣΔ) 10-1000 SPS 16-24 bit Audio, precision sensors
Dual-Slope 1-100 SPS 12-22 bit DMMs, low cost precision

Sampling Theory

Nyquist Criterion
fsample > 2 × fmax
Anti-Alias Filter
LP filter before ADC to remove f > fs/2
Aliasing: Frequencies above Nyquist fold back into the signal band. A 1kHz signal sampled at 800 SPS appears as 200Hz! Always use anti-alias filtering.
