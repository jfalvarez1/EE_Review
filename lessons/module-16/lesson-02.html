Headphone and Line Drivers

Driving headphones and line-level outputs requires different considerations. Headphones are low impedance (16-600Ω) requiring current drive, while line outputs need voltage drive into high impedance.

Headphone Amplifier Topologies

Topology Output Z Power Notes
Op-amp buffer <1Ω 10-50mW Simple, low power, use rail-to-rail
Diamond buffer <0.1Ω 50-500mW High current, very low distortion
Discrete Class A 1-10Ω 100mW-1W Warm sound, inefficient
Class AB push-pull <0.5Ω 100mW-2W Efficient, needs bias for crossover

Output Impedance Matters

Damping Factor
DF = Zload / Zout
Target DF
> 8 for tight bass control
High Output Z Problem: With high output impedance, the frequency response varies with headphone impedance curves. Many headphones have impedance peaks that will cause frequency response bumps.

DC Protection

Headphone outputs must be protected against DC offset which can damage drivers:

Coupling Capacitor
Blocks DC, adds low-freq rolloff
DC Servo
Integrator nulls offset, preserves bass
DC Detect + Relay
Monitors output, disconnects on fault
