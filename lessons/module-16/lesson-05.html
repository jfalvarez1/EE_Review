SAR ADC Architecture

The Successive Approximation Register (SAR) ADC is the workhorse of medium-speed, medium-resolution conversion. It uses a binary search algorithm to find the digital code that best represents the analog input, making it efficient in both power and silicon area.

SAR Characteristics

  • Resolution: 8-20 bits typical
  • Speed: 10 kSPS - 10 MSPS
  • Power: Very low (μW to mW)
  • Latency: 1 conversion cycle

Applications

  • Data acquisition: Precision measurement
  • Sensors: Temperature, pressure, etc.
  • Medical: ECG, patient monitoring
  • Industrial: Process control
