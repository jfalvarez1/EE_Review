Audio Tone Control (Baxandall)

The Baxandall tone control circuit provides symmetrical boost/cut of bass and treble frequencies with a flat response at center position. This topology has been the standard in audio equipment since the 1950s.

Baxandall Features

  • Symmetrical: Equal boost and cut
  • Low interaction: Bass/treble independent
  • Flat at center: Unity gain when centered
  • Range: ±12 to ±20 dB typical

Turnover Frequencies

  • Bass: 100-300 Hz
  • Treble: 2-5 kHz
  • Shelving: 6 dB/octave slopes
