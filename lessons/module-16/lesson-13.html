High-Speed Data Converter Considerations

High-speed data converters (>10 MSPS) face unique challenges including clock jitter, power supply coupling, PCB layout effects, and transmission line considerations. Success requires attention to analog, digital, and RF design principles.

Speed Challenges

  • Clock jitter: Limits SNR at high fin
  • Aperture jitter: Sampling uncertainty
  • Power supply: Switching transients
  • Crosstalk: Digital to analog coupling

Design Focus Areas

  • PCB layout: Ground planes, routing
  • Decoupling: Multi-frequency bypass
  • Clock: Low jitter, clean distribution
  • Terminations: Matched impedances
