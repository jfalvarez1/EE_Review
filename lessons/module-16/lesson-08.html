Diagnostic Strategy: The working left channel is your reference. Compare measurements point-by-point between channels. The difference reveals the fault location.

The Scenario

Equipment: Pioneer SA-7500 integrated amplifier (circa 1978)

  • Complaint: Distorted sound in right channel at all volume levels
  • History: Worked fine until recently, no recent service
  • Visual inspection: No obvious damage, all fuses OK
  • Initial test: Left channel clean, right channel crossover distortion

Root Cause Analysis

Finding: Bias transistor (VBE multiplier) has degraded.

  • Q3 (2N2222) measured: hFE=20 (spec: 75-300)
  • Leakage elevated, thermal runaway risk
  • Likely cause: Thermal cycling over 40+ years
  • Solution: Replace Q3 with matched transistor

Repair: Replace 2N2222, adjust bias pot for 15mA quiescent current per output transistor (measure 15mV across 1Ω emitter resistors).
