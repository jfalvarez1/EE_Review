Supervisory & Reset Circuits

Voltage supervisors monitor power rails and generate reset signals during power-up, brownout, and power-down. They ensure microcontrollers and logic start in known states.

Supervisor Selection Guide

Parameter Consideration
Threshold Accuracy ±1-2% typical, affects timing margin
Hysteresis 2-5% prevents chatter
Reset Delay Allow oscillator startup, typically 50-200ms
Active Low/High Match to MCU reset polarity
Manual Reset MR pin for button input
Common Mistake: Setting threshold too close to MCU minimum voltage leaves no margin for supply variation, temperature, or aging. Add 5-10% margin above datasheet minimum.
