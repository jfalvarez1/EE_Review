Multi-Rail Power Sequencing

Complex ICs like FPGAs and SoCs require multiple voltage rails that must power up in specific order. Incorrect sequencing can cause latch-up, excessive current, or damage.

Key Insight: I/O rails must not exceed core voltage during startup to prevent internal ESD structures from forward-biasing. Check the device datasheet for specific sequencing requirements.
