Audio Amplifier Design

This project combines analog design fundamentals with power electronics to create a high-fidelity audio amplifier. We'll explore Class AB output stages, biasing for low crossover distortion, feedback design for stability, and thermal management. These principles apply to headphone amplifiers, guitar amplifiers, and hi-fi systems.

Output Power:25W into 8Ω
THD+N:<0.01%
Bandwidth:20Hz - 20kHz (±0.5dB)
Topology:Class AB with VAS
Design Philosophy: Audio amplifier quality is measured by what it doesn't add to the signal - distortion, noise, and coloration. The key to low distortion is high open-loop gain combined with careful feedback design for stability.
