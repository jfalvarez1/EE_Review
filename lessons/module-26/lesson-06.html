High-Precision DAQ System

This project brings together precision analog design, digital conversion, signal conditioning, and noise management to create a multi-channel data acquisition system capable of measuring microvolt-level signals with sub-ppm accuracy. Such systems are used in scientific instrumentation, industrial process control, and medical devices.

Resolution:24-bit (16.7M levels)
Input Range:±10V, ±1V, ±100mV
Accuracy:<10 ppm
Noise Floor:<1 μVpp
Design Philosophy: In precision measurement, every component and layout decision affects accuracy. The analog front-end, reference, power supply, and grounding must all be designed as a coherent system, not individual blocks.
