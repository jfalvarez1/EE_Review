Sample and Hold Circuits

Sample-and-hold (S/H) circuits capture an analog voltage at a precise moment and hold it stable during ADC conversion. Critical for accurate sampling of dynamic signals.

Acquisition Time
tacq = N × τ (to settle within 1 LSB)
Aperture Jitter
Timing uncertainty in sample moment
Key Insight: The hold capacitor and switch resistance form an RC time constant. For N-bit accuracy, you need to wait for settling to <0.5 LSB, typically 7-10× the RC time constant.

S/H Error Sources

Error Cause Mitigation
Acquisition Error Insufficient settling time Wait for full RC settling
Charge Injection Switch capacitance couples to Chold Dummy switch, differential design
Droop Leakage discharges Chold Low leakage switch, larger C
Aperture Jitter Clock timing uncertainty Low-jitter clock source

Aperture Jitter Effects

Error Voltage
Verr = 2πf × Vpk × tjitter
For 12-bit @ 1MHz
Need tjitter < 77ps!
High Frequency Limitation: As signal frequency increases, even picoseconds of jitter cause significant errors. This often limits ENOB more than quantization noise at high frequencies.
