Capacitive Sensing

Capacitive sensors detect changes in capacitance caused by the proximity, position, or physical properties of objects. They are used for touch interfaces, proximity detection, level sensing, and precision displacement measurement. This lesson covers the principles and circuits for capacitive sensor interfaces.

Capacitance Range~1pF to ~100nF
ResolutionSub-femtofarad possible
ApplicationsTouch, Proximity, Level, Position
TechniquesCharge Transfer, Sigma-Delta, Relaxation
Key Principle: C = ε₀εᵣA/d. Capacitance depends on area (A), distance (d), and dielectric constant (εᵣ). Sensors exploit changes in any of these parameters.
