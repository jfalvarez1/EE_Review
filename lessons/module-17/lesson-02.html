Protection Network

ISO 7637-2 Test Pulses:
  • Pulse 1: -150V, 2ms (disconnection of inductive loads)
  • Pulse 2a: +37V, 50µs (ignition off)
  • Pulse 2b: +10V, 0.2ms (ignition on)
  • Pulse 3a/b: ±150V, 100ns superimposed (switching)
  • Pulse 5: Load dump +87V to +123V for 40-400ms
