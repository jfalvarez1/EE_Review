ADC Driver Circuits

The ADC input driver must provide adequate bandwidth, low noise, and sufficient current to charge the ADC's sample capacitor. Poor driver design is a common cause of ADC performance degradation.

Driver Requirements

  • Bandwidth: 3-5× signal bandwidth
  • Settling: Fast settling to N+2 bits
  • Drive: Charge sample capacitor
  • Noise: Lower than ADC quantization

Common Issues

  • Ringing: Underdamped RC
  • Distortion: Op-amp saturation
  • Noise: Wideband noise aliasing
  • Kickback: Charge injection
