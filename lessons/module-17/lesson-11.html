ADC Driver Circuits

The ADC input driver must provide adequate bandwidth, low noise, and sufficient current to charge the ADC's sample capacitor. Poor driver design is a common cause of ADC performance degradation.

Driver Requirements

  • Bandwidth: 3-5× signal bandwidth
  • Settling: Fast settling to N+2 bits
  • Drive: Charge sample capacitor
  • Noise: Lower than ADC quantization

Common Issues

  • Ringing: Underdamped RC
  • Distortion: Op-amp saturation
  • Noise: Wideband noise aliasing
  • Kickback: Charge injection

Differential ADC Driver

Single-ended to differential driver template: Rg1 = Rg2 and Rf1 = Rf2 set matched feedback ratios. OUT− feeds IN+ and OUT+ feeds IN−, providing negative differential feedback. VOCM controls output common mode; Rs1/Rs2 and Cdiff isolate ADC sampling kickback. Supplies, enable and optional common-mode capacitors are omitted. Choose values and verify settling, input common mode and ADC drive range using TI THS4551, ADC driving applications.
