MEMS Sensor Interfaces

Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS) sensors combine mechanical elements with electronic circuits on a single chip. They measure acceleration, rotation, pressure, and more with high precision in tiny packages. This lesson covers interfacing with common MEMS sensors including accelerometers, gyroscopes, and pressure sensors.

Common MEMSAccelerometer, Gyro, Barometer, Mic
InterfaceI2C, SPI, Analog
PowerµA to mA range
IntegrationIMU = Accel + Gyro + Mag
MEMS Advantage: Small size, low power, low cost, high integration. A 6-axis IMU (3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyroscope) fits in a 3×3mm package and costs under $2.
