High-Speed BJT Circuits

High-speed BJT circuits like ECL (Emitter-Coupled Logic) operate transistors in non-saturating mode to avoid storage time delays. Current-mode logic achieves sub-nanosecond switching by steering current between differential pairs rather than fully turning transistors on/off.

Why Non-Saturating?

Mode Issue Impact
Saturated Excess base charge stored Storage time delays (5-50ns typical)
Non-saturating BJT stays in forward-active No storage time, fast switching
Key Insight: ECL keeps transistors in forward-active mode by limiting voltage swing. The small ~800mV swing means no transistor ever saturates, eliminating storage time.
