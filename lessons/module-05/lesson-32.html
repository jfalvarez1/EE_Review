Precision BJT Matched Pairs

Matched transistor pairs are essential for precision analog circuits. They share the same silicon die, ensuring matched IS, β, and thermal tracking. Applications include differential pairs, current mirrors, and voltage references where VBE matching to sub-millivolt levels is required.
