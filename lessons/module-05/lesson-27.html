BJT SPICE Modeling

The device model, parameter by parameter

A simulator’s idea of a transistor is this list of numbers. Every one of them is a coefficient in an equation from this module, and reading them is a fast way to see what a model does and does not capture. Enter these in Circuit Toy or any solver that accepts a device model.
