Darlington and Sziklai Pairs

When a single transistor's β isn't enough, compound transistor configurations multiply current gain. These are essential for driving high-current loads from weak signals.

Darlington β
βtotal = β₁ × β₂ (up to 50,000+)
Trade-off
Higher VBE and VCE,sat
Industry Use: Relay drivers (ULN2003), motor control, touch sensors, audio power amps, high-current switches from logic signals.

Darlington Pair

Characteristics

Parameter Value
Total β β₁ × β₂ + β₁ + β₂
VBE ~1.2-1.4V (2 × VBE)
VCE,sat 0.9-2V (higher)
Switching Speed Slow (charge storage)
Leakage β₂ × ICBO1

Sziklai Pair (Complementary Darlington)

Advantages Over Darlington

  • Lower VBE: Only one VBE drop (~0.65V)
  • Lower VCE,sat: Output transistor not in saturation
  • Faster switching: Less charge storage
  • Better thermal stability: Complementary tempcos
Trade-off: Needs both NPN and PNP transistors with matched characteristics.

Speed-Up Resistor for Darlington

Add a resistor from Q2 base to emitter to speed up turn-off by providing a discharge path:

RBE Value
1kΩ - 10kΩ typical
Effect
Bleeds Q2 base charge, faster turn-off

Many integrated Darlingtons (TIP120, ULN2003) include this resistor internally.
