Power MOSFET Losses & Thermal Management

Power MOSFETs are efficient switches, but they still dissipate heat through conduction, switching, and gate-drive losses. If the junction temperature rises too high, RDS(on) increases, efficiency collapses, and reliability suffers.

Conduction loss
Pcond ≈ I2RDS(on)
Switching loss (rough)
Psw ≈ 0.5·V·I·(tr+tf)·fsw
Gate-drive loss
Pgate = QgVdrvfsw
Temperature rise
ΔT ≈ PtotRθJA
Practical workflow: estimate losses → compute junction temperature → check SOA/margins → iterate (device, gate drive, layout, heatsinking).
