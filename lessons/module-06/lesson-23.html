Application Selection Guide

Application Preferred Type Key Considerations
DC-DC Buck Switch N-FET (low-side) or both Fast switching, low Qg
Motor Driver H-Bridge 4× N-FET with gate drivers Shoot-through protection, dead-time
Load Switch P-FET (high-side) Simple gate drive, soft-start
Analog Multiplexer N-FET or transmission gate Low Ron, low charge injection
Reverse Polarity Protection P-FET with body diode Very low Vf when on

Body Diode Considerations

Every MOSFET has a parasitic body diode from source to drain (N-FET) or drain to source (P-FET):

Forward Drop
VF ≈ 0.7-1.2V
Reverse Recovery
trr = 50-500ns typical
Qrr (Recovery Charge)
10-500nC typical

Reverse Recovery Waveform

Body diode turns off slowly, causing reverse current spike (shoot-through risk)

Synchronous Rectification: In buck/boost converters, the body diode conducts during dead-time. Slow recovery causes losses. Use Schottky in parallel or fast-recovery MOSFETs.
