MOSFET Amplifiers

MOSFET amplifiers use the same topologies as BJT amplifiers but with different biasing requirements due to the square-law characteristic and high input impedance.

Common Source Gain
Av = −gm × RD
With Source Degeneration
Av = −gmRD/(1 + gmRS)
Key Difference: MOSFET gm is lower than BJT at same current, so gain is typically lower. However, the infinite input impedance makes biasing easier and loading negligible.

Common Source Amplifier Circuit

Key Equations

gm= 2√(K×ID) = 2K×VOV
Av= −gm × RD
Av (with RS)= −gmRD/(1+gmRS)
Zin= R1 ∥ R2 (very high)
Zout≈ RD
