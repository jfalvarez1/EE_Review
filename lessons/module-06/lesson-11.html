Power MOSFET Selection

Selecting the right power MOSFET for an output stage requires balancing multiple parameters: Rds(on), gate charge, voltage rating, package thermal capability, and cost. This lesson covers the key selection criteria and trade-offs.

Key Parameters

  • Vds(max): Drain-source voltage
  • Id(max): Continuous drain current
  • Rds(on): On-state resistance
  • Qg: Total gate charge

Trade-offs

  • Lower Rds(on): Higher Qg, larger die
  • Higher Vds: Higher Rds(on)
  • Better Tj: Larger package
  • Lower cost: Fewer features
