CMOS Inverter Fundamentals

The CMOS inverter is the core building block of digital logic. A PMOS pull-up and NMOS pull-down create a strong logic swing with (ideally) near-zero static current except during switching.

Static power (ideal)
Pstatic ≈ 0
Dynamic energy
E ≈ CLVDD2 (per 0→1 transition)
Dynamic power
P ≈ α CLVDD2f
Switching point
Set by NMOS/PMOS strength ratio
Analog view: Around the switching threshold, both devices conduct and the inverter behaves like a high-gain amplifier. This is why digital gates have “noise margins” and why short-circuit current exists during transitions.
