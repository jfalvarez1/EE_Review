Gate Drive Design (Qg, Rg, Miller)

The MOSFET gate is mostly a capacitor. Switching speed is set by how fast your driver can move charge into and out of the gate, and how you control dV/dt and ringing with gate resistance and layout.

Switching Time (rule-of-thumb)
t ≈ Qg / Idrv
Gate Drive Power
Pgate = Qg · Vdrv · fsw
Peak Gate Current (approx.)
Ipk ≈ Vdrv / Rg
Miller Plateau
Cgd couples drain dV/dt into gate
Practical rule: A fast MOSFET is a fast capacitor. If the driver is weak (or the loop inductance is high), your switching losses and EMI will be dominated by how you charge/discharge the gate.
