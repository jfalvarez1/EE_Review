Current Limiting and Protection

Output stages must be protected from overcurrent conditions that can damage transistors or loads. Current limiting can be continuous (foldback or constant) or fast-acting (for short-circuit protection). Understanding these protection schemes is essential for reliable power stage design.

Protection Types

  • Constant current: Hard limit at Imax
  • Foldback: Reduces current under fault
  • Hiccup mode: Cycles on/off in fault
  • Latched shutdown: Stays off until reset

Sensing Methods

  • Sense resistor: Precise, power loss
  • MOSFET Rds(on): Lossless, less precise
  • Current transformer: Isolated, AC only
  • Hall effect: Isolated, DC capable
