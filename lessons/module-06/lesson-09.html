MOSFET Current Mirrors (Biasing in ICs)

In analog ICs, current mirrors generate bias currents for differential pairs, active loads, and gain stages. MOSFET mirrors are compact and low-power, but real devices have finite output resistance and mismatch, so the mirrored current depends on VDS and device parameters.

Channel-length modulation
ID ≈ ID0(1 + λVDS)
Output resistance
ro ≈ 1 / (λID)
Compliance (simple mirror)
Vout,min ≈ Vov
Compliance (cascode mirror)
Vout,min ≈ 2·Vov
Design intuition: A simple mirror is usually “good enough” for biasing, but when you need high gain (large ro) you cascode the mirror to reduce VDS variation on the mirroring device.

Schematics

Simple NMOS current mirror: M1 is diode-connected; M2 mirrors the current into the output node.

Cascode mirror: adds two devices to boost output resistance, at the cost of higher required Vout.
