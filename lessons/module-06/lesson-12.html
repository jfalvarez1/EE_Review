IGBT Selection and Drive

Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistors (IGBTs) combine the high input impedance of MOSFETs with the low saturation voltage of bipolar transistors. They dominate high-power applications from motor drives to welders. This lesson covers IGBT selection criteria and gate drive requirements.

IGBT Advantages

  • Low Vce(sat): ~2V at high current
  • High voltage: 600V to 6.5kV
  • Easy gate drive: Voltage controlled
  • High current: 100A+ per device

IGBT Limitations

  • Tail current: Slow turn-off
  • Vce(sat) drop: Limits efficiency at low power
  • No body diode: Need external diode
  • Lower frequency: Typically <50kHz
