Output Protection Circuits

Output stages must survive short circuits, overloads, and reverse voltages. Protection circuits limit stress while allowing normal operation—critical for reliable products.

Current Limiting
Imax = Vsense / Rsense
Foldback Limiting
Reduces Ilimit as Vout drops
Key Insight: Simple current limiting still allows high power dissipation (Isupply × Vdrop). Foldback reduces current as output shorts, limiting power in the output device.

Protection Methods

Method Mechanism Pros/Cons
Sense Resistor Voltage drop triggers limit Simple, adds loss
Foldback Reduces Ilim as Vout drops Low SOA stress, can latch
Hiccup/Restart Shuts off, retries periodically Lowest dissipation, glitchy
Thermal Shutdown Monitors die temperature Ultimate protection, needs reset

Reverse Voltage Protection

Series Diode
Simple, drops Vf, wastes power
P-FET (ideal diode)
Low loss, turns off for reverse
TVS/Clamp
Limits voltage transients
ESD Protection: Outputs exposed to the outside world need ESD protection. TVS diodes or internal clamps shunt ESD strikes to the rails.
