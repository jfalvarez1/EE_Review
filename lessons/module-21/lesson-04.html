Power Supply Noise & PSRR

Power supply rejection ratio (PSRR) determines how much supply noise appears at the output. Understanding PSRR variation with frequency is critical for sensitive analog circuits.

Key Insight: PSRR degrades at high frequency. A 100dB PSRR at DC may be only 30dB at 100kHz. This means switching regulator noise at 500kHz passes through with minimal attenuation.
