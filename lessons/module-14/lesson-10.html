Analog Computation Circuits

Analog computers use op-amps and passive components to perform mathematical operations in real-time. While digital dominates general computing, analog circuits excel at continuous-time signal processing, solving differential equations, and simulating physical systems.

Key Insight: Analog integrators with a 10kΩ/1µF RC can integrate signals from DC to ~100Hz with <1% error. They're ideal for control systems and sensor signal conditioning where DSP latency is unacceptable.

Solving Differential Equations

Analog computers excel at solving differential equations in real-time. Example: Second-order system (mass-spring-damper):

Equation: m(d²x/dt²) + b(dx/dt) + kx = F(t)

Each integrator corresponds to one state variable (velocity, position).
