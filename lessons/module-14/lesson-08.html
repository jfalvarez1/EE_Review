Voltage-Controlled Oscillators and Filters

Voltage-controlled oscillators (VCOs) and filters (VCFs) are fundamental building blocks in PLLs, frequency synthesizers, and analog signal processing. Understanding their transfer functions and nonlinearities is essential for system-level design.

Key Insight: VCO phase noise degrades with higher KVCO because control voltage noise directly modulates frequency. For low phase noise, use smaller tuning range with coarse/fine tuning stages.
