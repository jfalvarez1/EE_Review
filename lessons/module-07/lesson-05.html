RIAA Phono Preamplifier

Vinyl records are cut with RIAA equalization - bass reduced and treble boosted during recording. The phono preamp must apply the inverse curve while providing 40-60dB of gain for the tiny cartridge signals.

RIAA Time Constants

  • τ1: 3180 μs (50.05 Hz)
  • τ2: 318 μs (500.5 Hz)
  • τ3: 75 μs (2122 Hz)

Cartridge Types

  • MM (Moving Magnet): 2-8 mV output
  • MC (Moving Coil): 0.2-0.5 mV output
  • Load: 47kΩ || 100-200pF (MM)

RIAA Equalization Curve

The RIAA curve provides 20dB of bass boost at 20Hz and 20dB of treble cut at 20kHz relative to 1kHz.

RIAA Phono Preamp Circuit

A typical inverting RIAA preamp uses a frequency-dependent feedback network to implement the equalization.
