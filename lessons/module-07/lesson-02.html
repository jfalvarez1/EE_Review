Class AB Push-Pull Output Stages

Class AB combines the low distortion of Class A with the efficiency of Class B. A small bias current keeps both output transistors slightly conducting, eliminating crossover distortion.

Max Efficiency
ηmax = 78.5% (π/4)
Bias Current
IQ = 1-10% of Ipeak
Key Insight: The NPN handles positive half-cycles, PNP handles negative. At crossover, both are slightly on due to bias. This "Class AB" operating region smooths the transition.
