Audio Grounding and Shielding

Proper grounding and shielding are critical for low-noise audio systems. Ground loops, electromagnetic interference (EMI), and electrostatic pickup can introduce hum, buzz, and noise that degrade audio quality.

Noise Sources

  • 50/60 Hz hum: Magnetic coupling from mains
  • 120 Hz buzz: Ground loops, rectifier ripple
  • RF interference: Radio, cell phones, WiFi
  • Electrostatic: Capacitive coupling

Solutions

  • Star grounding: Single-point ground
  • Shielded cables: Electrostatic protection
  • Balanced lines: Common-mode rejection
  • Ground lift: Break ground loops
