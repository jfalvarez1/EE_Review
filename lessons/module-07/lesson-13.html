Parallel and Series Connected Devices

When a single transistor cannot handle the required current or voltage, multiple devices can be connected in parallel or series. This lesson covers the techniques, challenges, and balancing methods for multi-device configurations.

Parallel Connection

  • Purpose: Increase current capacity
  • Challenge: Current sharing
  • Key: Match Vth and Rds(on)
  • Benefit: Reduces total Rds(on)

Series Connection

  • Purpose: Increase voltage rating
  • Challenge: Voltage sharing
  • Key: Static and dynamic balancing
  • Benefit: Higher Vds capability
