Audio Dynamics Processing

Dynamics processors control the amplitude of audio signals based on their level. Compressors, limiters, expanders, and gates are essential tools in recording, broadcast, and live sound to manage dynamic range and prevent clipping.

Processor Types

  • Compressor: Reduces dynamic range above threshold
  • Limiter: Hard ceiling (∞:1 ratio)
  • Expander: Increases dynamic range below threshold
  • Gate: Mutes below threshold

Key Parameters

  • Threshold: Level where processing begins
  • Ratio: Input/output change (2:1 to ∞:1)
  • Attack: Response to level increase
  • Release: Recovery time after level drops
