Totem-Pole and H-Bridge Drivers

Totem-pole outputs stack complementary transistors to drive loads to either rail. H-bridges use four switches to enable bidirectional current flow, essential for motor control and full-bridge converters. Understanding shoot-through prevention and dead-time is critical for reliable operation.

Totem-Pole Features

  • Active pull-up/down: Fast transitions
  • Low output impedance: Both states
  • Rail-to-rail swing: Nearly full Vcc
  • Shoot-through risk: During transitions

H-Bridge Applications

  • DC motor control: Forward/reverse
  • Stepper motors: Bipolar drive
  • Full-bridge inverters: AC output
  • Audio Class D: BTL output
