Balanced Audio Design

Balanced audio uses differential signaling to achieve superior noise rejection over long cable runs. Professional audio equipment universally uses balanced connections (XLR, TRS) for microphones, line-level signals, and AES/EBU digital audio.

Balanced Advantages

  • CMRR: 40-80 dB noise rejection
  • Long runs: 100+ meter capability
  • Lower crosstalk: Opposite phase cancels
  • Standard: XLR, TRS, AES3

Level Standards

  • +4 dBu: Professional (1.228 Vrms)
  • -10 dBV: Consumer (0.316 Vrms)
  • 0 dBu: Reference (0.775 Vrms)
  • Headroom: +20 to +24 dBu max
