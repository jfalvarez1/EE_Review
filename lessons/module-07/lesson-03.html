Precision Analog Techniques

Precision analog circuits must minimize DC errors like offset voltage and drift. Techniques like chopper stabilization and auto-zero eliminate these errors at the cost of complexity and bandwidth.

Key Insight: Chopper amplifiers modulate the input signal to a higher frequency where 1/f noise is negligible, amplify it, then demodulate back. This effectively shifts the DC errors to the chopper frequency where they can be filtered out.
