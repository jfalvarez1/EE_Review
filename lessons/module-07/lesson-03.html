Rail-to-Rail Output Stages

Rail-to-rail output (RRO) stages can swing the output voltage to within millivolts of the supply rails. This maximizes usable signal range, especially important for low-voltage designs.

Saturation Voltage
Vsat = 20-100mV typical (RRO)
vs Standard
Vsat = 1-2V (emitter follower)
Key Insight: Rail-to-rail outputs use common-emitter (or common-source) configurations that saturate transistors at the rails, rather than emitter followers that can't go closer than Vbe to the rail.

Trade-offs

Output Impedance
RRO has higher Zout near rails
Linearity
Distortion increases at extremes
Load Sensitivity
Vsat increases with load current
Near-Rail Behavior: Op-amp specs often show Vsat at light load. Heavy loads push the output away from rails. Check datasheet graphs for your actual conditions.
