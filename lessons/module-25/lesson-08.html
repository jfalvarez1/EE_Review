Sensor Interface Design

Sensors convert physical quantities into electrical signals that require careful analog signal conditioning before digitization. This lesson covers common sensor types and their interface circuits for industrial, automotive, and consumer applications.

Industry Reference: Based on Analog Devices RTD Measurement, TI Precision Labs - Sensors, and Sensor Signal Chain Design.

Section 1: RTD Temperature Measurement

RTDs (Resistance Temperature Detectors) are highly accurate temperature sensors. PT100 has 100Ω at 0°C; PT1000 has 1000Ω at 0°C.

Section 2: Thermocouple Interface

Thermocouples generate a voltage proportional to temperature difference between hot and cold junctions. Cold junction compensation (CJC) is required.

Section 3: Strain Gauge / Load Cell

Strain gauges measure deformation by resistance change. Typical gauge factor is 2, meaning ΔR/R = 2 × strain.

Section 4: Pressure Sensor Interface

MEMS pressure sensors typically output mV-level signals that require amplification. Many modern sensors include on-chip signal conditioning.
