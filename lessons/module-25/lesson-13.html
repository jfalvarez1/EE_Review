Why 48V in Automotive?

The automotive industry is transitioning from 12V-only to 48V/12V dual systems:

Factor12V System48V SystemBenefit
Max Power (practical)~3 kW~15 kW5× more electrical power
Current @ 3kW250A62.5A4× smaller cables
Cable weightBaseline-75%Fuel savings
Touch-safe?YesYes (<60V DC)No HV isolation needed
Motor efficiencyLowerHigherBetter fuel economy

48V Enables:

  • Mild Hybrid (MHEV): 10-15kW motor/generator for start-stop, regenerative braking
  • Electric Supercharger: Instant boost without turbo lag
  • Electric A/C Compressor: Climate control independent of engine
  • Active Suspension: High-power actuators for ride control
  • Heated Catalyst: Faster emissions compliance at cold start
LV 148 Standard: 48V automotive systems follow LV 148 (German OEM standard) defining voltage ranges, transients, and safety requirements. Nominal is 48V, operating range is 36-52V, transients up to 70V.

Active Discharge: 48V systems must discharge bus capacitors to <30V within 1 second after key-off or fault. This typically requires a dedicated discharge resistor with MOSFET switch.

EMC Considerations

48V systems create EMC challenges due to high switching currents:

48V-Specific EMC Challenges

  • Higher di/dt: 48V inverters switch 200A+ at 20kHz → high frequency noise
  • Longer cables: 48V wiring acts as antenna
  • Ground loops: Between 48V and 12V grounds
  • DC-DC converter: Major noise source, needs careful filtering
