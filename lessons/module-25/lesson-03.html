Capacitive Touch Sensing

Capacitive touch sensing is ubiquitous in smartphones, laptops, appliances, and automotive interfaces. Understanding the analog front-end (AFE) that detects tiny capacitance changes (femtofarads to picofarads) is essential for embedded design.

Capacitive Sensing Fundamentals

A touch sensor electrode has a baseline capacitance (Cp) to ground. When a finger approaches, it adds touch capacitance (Ct) in parallel, increasing total capacitance by 0.1-5pF typically.

Capacitance-to-Voltage AFE

A typical capacitive sensing AFE uses a switched-capacitor integrator with synchronous demodulation to reject noise.

Key AFE Specifications:
  • Resolution: 0.1 fF typical (16-bit ADC after gain)
  • Scan rate: 50-200 Hz per sensor
  • SNR: > 60 dB to reject environmental noise
  • Excitation frequency: 50kHz - 500kHz (avoid 50/60Hz and harmonics)

Noise Sources and Mitigation

Common Noise Sources

Noise Source Frequency Mechanism Mitigation
AC Mains 50/60 Hz + harmonics Capacitive coupling to body Sync demod, notch filter, spread spectrum
LCD noise ~15-75 kHz Backlight PWM, row drivers Frequency hopping, time-division
Charger noise 50-500 kHz SMPS ripple on body Higher excitation freq, averaging
RF interference MHz - GHz EMI pickup on traces Shielding, differential sensing
Thermal noise Broadband Johnson noise in resistors Low R, oversampling, averaging

Spread Spectrum Excitation

Instead of a fixed excitation frequency, spread spectrum varies the frequency randomly within a band. This spreads noise energy and avoids synchronization with interfering sources.

Capacitive Touch Design Checklist

Progressive Exercises

Level 1: Fundamentals

Exercise 1: A touch sensor has 15pF parasitic capacitance. A finger touch adds 1.5pF. What is the percentage change in capacitance?

ΔC/Cp × 100%

Exercise 2: A charge transfer circuit uses Cint = 100pF, VDD = 3.3V, and runs 8 cycles. If Cx = 12pF, what is the final voltage on Cint?

Vint = N × Cx × VDD / Cint

Level 2: Application

Exercise 3: A touch controller baseline reads 2000 counts. Touch threshold is set to 12%. What count value triggers a touch?

threshold_count = baseline × (1 + threshold%)

Exercise 4: An electrode over 2mm acrylic (εr = 3.0) has 12mm diameter. Estimate the parasitic capacitance to a ground plane directly below. (C = ε₀εrA/d)

A = π×(6mm)², d = 2mm, ε₀ = 8.854e-12 F/m

Level 3: System Design

Exercise 5: A touch sensor needs 100 samples/second scan rate with 10 channels. If each charge transfer needs 20 cycles at 200kHz excitation, what percentage of time is spent scanning?

Time per scan = 10 × 20/200kHz. Scans per second × time per scan / 1s
