Industrial 4-20mA Current Loop Design

Robust process control signaling for industrial environments

Why 4-20mA Current Loops?

In industrial environments, voltage signaling fails due to:

  • Long cable runs (100-1000m) with significant IR drop
  • Electrical noise from motors, VFDs, welders
  • Ground loops between equipment at different potentials
  • Cable resistance variations with temperature

Current signaling solves these problems:

PropertyVoltage SignalCurrent Loop
Cable resistance effectLarge error (100Ω = 2% at 5V/50mA)None (same current everywhere)
Noise immunityPoor (noise adds to signal)Excellent (noise is common-mode)
Wire break detectionNot possible0mA = fault detected
Ground loop immunityPoorExcellent with isolated receiver

Why 4mA Offset?

The 4mA zero offset provides:

  • Wire break detection: 0mA = fault, 4mA = valid zero
  • Self-powered transmitters: 4mA can power the sensor (≈ 96mW at 24V)
  • Diagnostic headroom: Below 4mA signals underrange/fault

Intrinsic Safety Considerations

In hazardous areas (Zone 0, 1, 2), current loops must be intrinsically safe:

IS Barrier Types

Barrier TypeVoltage DropIsolationUse Case
Zener BarrierLow (0.5-2V)None (galvanic)Simple, low cost
Galvanic Isolator2-5VFull isolationGround loops, noise
Active Isolator0-3VFull + powerLong loops, signal conditioning

Entity Parameters

  • Voc: Open circuit voltage (max at barrier output)
  • Isc: Short circuit current (max at barrier output)
  • Ca, La: Allowed cable capacitance/inductance
  • Vmax, Imax: Max device can receive safely
