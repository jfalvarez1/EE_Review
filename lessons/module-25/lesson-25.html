Medical Analog Front-End (ECG/PPG)

Medical devices require specialized analog front-ends (AFE) to acquire tiny biopotential signals from the human body. ECG (electrocardiogram) and PPG (photoplethysmography) are foundational biosignal types found in wearables, patient monitors, and diagnostic equipment. This lesson covers the circuit design challenges unique to medical applications.

Industry Applications

  • Wearable Health Monitors - Smartwatches, fitness bands
  • Patient Monitors - Hospital bedside units
  • Diagnostic ECG - 12-lead clinical systems
  • Pulse Oximeters - SpO2 measurement
  • Implantable Devices - Pacemakers, ICDs

2. ECG Front-End Architecture

Key Design Blocks

Instrumentation Amplifier (INA)

  • Very high input impedance (>10 GΩ)
  • High CMRR (>100 dB at DC, >80 dB at 50/60 Hz)
  • Low noise (<1 µVpp in 0.1-10Hz band)
  • Examples: AD8421, INA333, MAX30003 (integrated AFE)

Right Leg Drive (RLD)

  • Senses common-mode signal
  • Inverts and feeds back to body via RL electrode
  • Reduces effective CM voltage seen by INA
  • Improves CMRR by 20-40 dB

CMRR Requirement

With 1.5V CM signal and 1mV ECG, to keep CM interference < 10µV:

\[ CMRR > 20\log_{10}\left(\frac{1.5V}{10\mu V}\right) = 103 \text{ dB} \]

3. PPG Signal Acquisition

Photoplethysmography measures blood volume changes using light absorption:

PPG Principle

  • LED illuminates tissue (red ~660nm, IR ~940nm)
  • Photodiode detects transmitted/reflected light
  • Pulsatile blood flow modulates light absorption
  • AC component (pulsatile) carries heart rate info
  • Ratio of red/IR gives SpO2 (oxygen saturation)

PPG Front-End Design

Component Function Key Specs
LED Driver Pulsed current source, multiplexed R/IR 10-50mA peak, fast settling
Photodiode Light to current conversion Large area, low dark current
TIA Current to voltage conversion Low noise, high bandwidth
Ambient Light Reject Cancel DC from room lighting Synchronous sampling or modulation
ADC Digitize signal 18+ bits for AC/DC ratio

4. Integrated AFE Solutions

Modern medical AFEs integrate most analog signal chain in a single chip:

Part Application Features Power
MAX30003 ECG Single-lead, integrated RLD, 85µW Ultra-low power
ADS1292R ECG 2-ch, 24-bit, respiration detection 335µW/ch
MAX30102 PPG (SpO2) Integrated LED driver + PD + ADC 600µA active
AFE4900 ECG + PPG Combo biosensor AFE Configurable

Integrated vs Discrete Trade-offs

  • Integrated: Smaller, faster design, optimized for application
  • Discrete: More flexibility, easier to debug, can optimize each stage
  • For learning: Start with integrated AFE, understand signal chain
  • For production: Integrated preferred for size/power

5. Safety and Regulatory

Patient Safety (IEC 60601-1)

  • Patient Leakage Current: < 10µA normal, < 50µA fault
  • Isolation: 4kV for CF (cardiac floating) devices
  • Defibrillator Protection: Withstand 5kV, 360J discharge

Critical Safety Requirements

  • Use medical-rated isolation (reinforced insulation)
  • Include input protection networks for defibrillator events
  • Current-limit all electrode connections
  • Fail-safe design: Fault conditions must not harm patient
  • EMC compliance: IEC 60601-1-2 for immunity

6. Design Checklist

ECG Design

  • ☐ INA with CMRR > 100dB at DC, >80dB at line frequency
  • ☐ Input impedance > 10MΩ (ideally > 1GΩ)
  • ☐ Noise < 10µVpp in ECG band
  • ☐ Right leg drive for improved CMRR
  • ☐ High-pass filter (0.05-0.5Hz) for DC rejection
  • ☐ Low-pass filter (40-150Hz depending on application)
  • ☐ Notch filter for 50/60Hz if needed

PPG Design

  • ☐ LED driver with precise current control
  • ☐ Ambient light cancellation
  • ☐ High dynamic range for small AC/large DC
  • ☐ Synchronized sampling with LED pulses

Safety

  • ☐ Patient leakage current within limits
  • ☐ Appropriate isolation for device classification
  • ☐ Defibrillator protection on electrode inputs
  • ☐ ESD protection for user contact
