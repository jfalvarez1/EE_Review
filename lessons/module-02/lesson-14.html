Common Design Mistakes to Avoid

Circuit Common Mistake Solution
TIA Ignoring photodiode capacitance → oscillation Add feedback capacitor Cf for compensation
Instrumentation Amp Gain resistor too small → common-mode issues Keep Rg > 100Ω; check CMRR at gain
Peak Detector High leakage → excessive droop Use JFET-input buffer, film capacitor
Log Amplifier No temperature compensation → drift Use matched transistors or IC with internal comp
Gyrator Assuming floating inductor behavior Basic gyrator is grounded; use GIC for floating
Sample & Hold Charge injection causing offset Use dummy switch or bottom-plate sampling
V-to-I Exceeding compliance voltage Verify V_supply > I_max × R_load + headroom
Window Comparator Chattering at thresholds Add hysteresis to both comparators
Oscillator Amplitude clipping → distortion Use AGC (lamp, JFET, or dedicated IC)
PLL Loop bandwidth too wide → noise; too narrow → slow lock Simulate/optimize for jitter vs. lock time
