UART RS-232/RS-485 Transceivers

UART serial communication requires level translation and line driving for reliable long-distance communication. RS-232 and RS-485 define different electrical standards for different applications.

RS-232
Point-to-point, ±3-15V levels
RS-485
Multi-drop, differential ±1.5-6V
Max Distance
RS-232: 15m, RS-485: 1200m
Key Insight: RS-232 uses single-ended signaling with large voltage swings for noise immunity over short distances. RS-485 uses differential signaling which cancels common-mode noise, enabling much longer cables and higher speeds.

RS-232 vs RS-485 Comparison

Parameter RS-232 RS-485
Signaling Single-ended Differential
Voltage Levels ±3V to ±15V ±1.5V to ±6V differential
Topology Point-to-point Multi-drop (32-256 nodes)
Max Distance 15m @ 20kbps 1200m @ 100kbps
Max Speed ~1 Mbps 10 Mbps @ 12m
CMR N/A -7V to +12V typical

RS-485 Design Considerations

Termination
120Ω at each far end of the cable
Bias Resistors
Keep bus in known state when idle
Cable Type
Twisted pair, shielded if noisy
Fail-Safe
Pull A high, B low when open
Baud Rate vs Distance: The product of baud rate (bps) × cable length (m) should not exceed approximately 10⁸. At 1 Mbps, max distance ≈ 100m. At 115200 bps, max ≈ 870m.
