SPI Signal Integrity

SPI runs at higher speeds than I2C (up to 100MHz+) using push-pull drivers. This brings transmission line effects, crosstalk, and EMI challenges that require careful PCB design.

Key Insight: Unlike I2C, SPI uses push-pull drivers providing fast, defined edge rates. At speeds above ~10MHz, trace lengths become significant fractions of wavelength and require controlled impedance and termination.

PCB Design Guidelines

Trace Length
Keep < λ/10 for no termination
Impedance
Match driver Z to trace Z0
Length Matching
Match SCLK, MOSI, MISO within 5mm
Ground Return
Continuous ground plane under traces
Termination Options:
  • Series: 22-33Ω at driver output, matches Z0
  • Parallel: Z0 to ground at receiver, high power
  • AC (RC): Series R + shunt C, lower power
