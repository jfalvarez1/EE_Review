RS-485 Differential Signaling

RS-485 is a robust differential signaling standard designed for long-distance, multi-drop communication in noisy industrial environments. Its differential architecture provides excellent common-mode noise rejection.

Voltage Range
-7V to +12V
Differential
VA - VB ≥ ±200mV
Max Nodes
32 unit loads (standard)
Max Length
1200m at 100kbps
Key Insight: RS-485 uses differential signaling where the data is encoded as the voltage difference between two wires (A and B). Common-mode noise affects both wires equally and cancels out at the receiver, enabling reliable communication in electrically noisy environments.

Ground Potential Difference: RS-485 allows up to ±7V ground potential difference between nodes. Always connect signal ground between nodes to provide a common reference, even though the differential signaling rejects common-mode noise.
