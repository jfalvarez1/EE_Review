Scenario: Audio DAC Output Stage

Design the analog output stage for a high-fidelity audio DAC. Balance between low distortion, low noise, and ability to drive headphones or line outputs.

Op-Amp Selection Criteria

Parameter Requirement Why
Voltage noise <5 nV/√Hz Sets noise floor
THD <0.001% @ 1kHz Audible distortion
GBW >10 MHz Bandwidth with gain
Slew rate >10 V/µs No slew limiting at 20kHz
Output current >50 mA Drive low-Z loads
PSRR >80 dB Reject supply noise
Headphone Considerations:
  • 32Ω headphones @ 2Vrms need 62mA peak current
  • Op-amp must remain linear at this current
  • Consider dedicated headphone amp IC or discrete buffer
  • DC servo or coupling cap to block offset
