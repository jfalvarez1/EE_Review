Ferrite Beads & EMI Filtering

Ferrite beads provide frequency-dependent impedance for EMI filtering. Unlike inductors, they dissipate high-frequency energy as heat rather than storing it.

Key Insight: Ferrite beads are specified by impedance at 100 MHz, not inductance. Choose based on the noise frequency you want to attenuate, and verify DC resistance doesn't cause unacceptable voltage drop.
