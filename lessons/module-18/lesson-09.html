Hall Effect Sensors

Hall effect sensors detect magnetic fields by measuring the voltage developed across a current-carrying conductor when placed in a magnetic field. They are widely used for position sensing, current measurement, and speed detection. This lesson covers Hall sensor principles and interface circuits.

Hall VoltageV_H = K_H × I × B
Typical Sensitivity1-10 mV/mT
ApplicationsCurrent, Position, Speed, Proximity
TypesSwitch, Latch, Linear, Angle
Hall Effect Principle: When current flows through a conductor in a magnetic field, the Lorentz force deflects charge carriers, creating a voltage perpendicular to both the current and magnetic field.
