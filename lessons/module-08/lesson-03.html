Crystal Oscillators

Quartz crystals provide exceptional frequency stability with Q factors exceeding 10,000. They act as highly selective resonators with two resonance modes: series and parallel.

Crystal Q Factor
Q = 10,000 - 1,000,000
Frequency Stability
±10 - 100 ppm typical
Key Insight: Series resonance (fs) occurs where the motional L and C resonate. Parallel resonance (fp) is slightly higher, where the crystal looks inductive. Most oscillators use parallel mode with external load capacitors.

Crystal Selection Guidelines

Drive Level

Aging
