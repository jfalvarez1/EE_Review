Real-Time Clocks and Timekeeping

Real-Time Clocks (RTCs) maintain calendar and time information even when the main system is powered off. This lesson covers RTC architectures, crystal selection, and backup power considerations.

RTC Features

  • Calendar: Year/month/day/hour/min/sec
  • Alarms: Wake-up triggers
  • Battery backup: Coin cell power
  • Oscillator: 32.768 kHz crystal

Key Specifications

  • Accuracy: ±2-20 ppm typical
  • Current: 200-500 nA typical
  • Interface: I2C or SPI
  • Backup: Years on coin cell
