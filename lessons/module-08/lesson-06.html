Analog Multiplexers

Analog multiplexers (mux) route one of several analog signals to a single output, enabling multi-channel data acquisition with a single ADC. Understanding their limitations—on-resistance, charge injection, and bandwidth—is critical for precision applications.

Key Insight: RON variation with signal voltage (RON flatness) causes distortion. Low-voltage CMOS muxes can have 2:1 RON variation across the signal range. Use rail-to-rail or charge-pump enhanced muxes for precision.
