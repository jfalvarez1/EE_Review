Data Converter Specifications

Understanding datasheet specifications is crucial for selecting the right ADC or DAC for an application. Static specs (DNL, INL) and dynamic specs (SNR, SFDR, ENOB) characterize different aspects of converter performance.

Static Specifications

  • DNL: Differential nonlinearity
  • INL: Integral nonlinearity
  • Offset: Zero-code error
  • Gain: Full-scale error

Dynamic Specifications

  • SNR: Signal-to-noise ratio
  • SFDR: Spurious-free dynamic range
  • THD: Total harmonic distortion
  • ENOB: Effective number of bits
