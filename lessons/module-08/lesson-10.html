Clock Domain Crossing (CDC)

Moving signals between different clock domains is a critical challenge in digital design. Improper handling leads to metastability, data corruption, and intermittent failures. This lesson covers safe CDC techniques.

CDC Challenges

  • Metastability: Flip-flop indecision
  • Data loss: Fast→slow crossing
  • Data corruption: Multi-bit coherency
  • Glitches: Combinational logic hazards

Safe Solutions

  • Synchronizers: 2-3 flip-flop chain
  • Handshake: Request/acknowledge
  • FIFOs: Async buffer with pointers
  • Gray code: Single-bit changes
