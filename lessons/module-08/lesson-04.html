Log/Antilog Amplifiers

Logarithmic amplifiers exploit the exponential I-V characteristic of diodes or transistors to perform analog computation. They enable multiplication, division, and compression of wide dynamic range signals.

Log Amp
Vout = K × ln(Vin/Vref)
Antilog
Vout = Vref × e(Vin/K)
Dynamic Range
60-120 dB typical
Temperature
Must compensate VT = kT/q
Key Insight: The transistor's IC = IS × e(VBE/VT) relationship provides the logarithmic function. VT = 26mV at 25°C varies with temperature, requiring matched transistors and compensation circuits.
