Anti-Aliasing and Reconstruction Filters

The Nyquist theorem requires signals be band-limited to less than half the sample rate to prevent aliasing. Anti-aliasing filters before ADCs and reconstruction filters after DACs are essential components in any sampled-data system.

Aliasing Problem

  • Nyquist: fs > 2 × fmax
  • Aliasing: High frequencies fold back
  • fAlias: |fin - n×fs|
  • Irreversible: Cannot be removed after sampling

Filter Requirements

  • Passband: Flat to signal bandwidth
  • Stopband: Attenuate beyond fs/2
  • Transition: Sharp rolloff needed
  • Phase: Linear phase for time-domain
