Peak Detectors

Peak detectors capture and hold the maximum (or minimum) value of a signal. They're essential in envelope detection, automatic gain control, and signal measurement. Understanding the trade-offs between response speed and droop rate is key to proper design.

Key Insight: Simple diode peak detectors have a threshold of ~0.6V. Precision peak detectors use an op-amp to eliminate diode drop, but the op-amp slew rate limits high-frequency performance. For RF applications, consider Schottky diodes with lower VF.

Peak Detector Topologies

Topology Accuracy Speed Application
Simple Diode VF error (~0.6V) Very fast (ns) RF envelope, AGC
Op-amp + Diode ~mV (limited by offset) Medium (µs) Audio peak meters
Buffered Precision <1mV Slow (µs-ms) Instrumentation
Dual-Comparator Sub-mV Fast ADC peak detect

Design Equations

Droop Rate: dV/dt = Ileak / Chold

Ripple (half-wave): Vripple ≈ Vpk / (f × Rload × C)

Attack Time: τattack = Rsource × Chold

Decay Time: τdecay = Rload × Chold

Peak Error (simple): ε = VF + IR × Rdiode
