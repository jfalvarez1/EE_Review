Sample and Hold Circuits

Sample and Hold (S/H) circuits capture an analog voltage at a precise moment and hold it steady for processing. They're essential for ADC interfaces where the input must remain stable during conversion.

Acquisition Time
Time to capture input to accuracy
Hold Droop
Voltage decay during hold
Aperture Jitter
Timing uncertainty at sample
Feedthrough
Input coupling during hold
Key Insight: The fundamental trade-off is capacitor size: large C means low droop but slow acquisition; small C gives fast acquisition but high droop. Aperture jitter becomes critical at high frequencies—1ns jitter at 1MHz causes 0.6% error.
