Common Power Problems

Symptom Likely Cause Debug Action
Large ripple at fsw Insufficient output capacitance Add bulk capacitor, check ESR
Spikes at switching edges Poor layout, high ESL Add ceramic caps near switcher
LF oscillation Compensation issue, ESR too low Check feedback network, add ESR
Voltage droop under load Inadequate regulation, trace resistance Measure at load, check feedback
Startup overshoot Soft-start too fast Increase soft-start cap
Beware Ground Bounce: If you see noise that correlates with digital activity, you may be measuring ground bounce, not actual supply noise. Probe between VCC and local GND.
