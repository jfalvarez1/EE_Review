Reverse Polarity Protection

Reverse polarity protection prevents damage when power is connected backwards. Methods range from simple diodes to ideal diode controllers.

Series Diode
Simple, ~0.5V drop
Series P-FET
Low drop (~50mV)
Ideal Diode IC
Best: ~20mV drop
Key Trade-off: Simple protection (diode) has high voltage drop and power loss. FET-based solutions are more efficient but need proper gate drive and protection for the gate.

P-FET Protection Circuit

Normal Operation (V_IN +)

  • Gate pulled to GND through R
  • VGS = -VIN → FET fully ON
  • Very low voltage drop: I × RDS(on)

Reversed (V_IN −)

  • Body diode blocks reverse current
  • FET is OFF (VGS ≈ 0)
  • Circuit protected from damage
Gate Protection: Add a Zener (12-15V) to clamp VGS if input can exceed FET's VGS(max). Gate oxide is fragile!

Real-World Applications

Reverse polarity protection is critical in many applications. Here are 10 real-world scenarios:

1. Automotive 12V Systems

Jump-start cables connected backwards can destroy ECUs. Use P-FET with 30mΩ RDS(on) for 5-10A loads. Add TVS for load dump protection (60V transients).

2. Battery-Powered Devices

Consumer devices with battery compartments. Schottky diodes work for <500mA; FET for higher currents. Consider polarity-forcing battery contacts as mechanical protection.

3. Solar Panel Connections

Panels connected in wrong orientation. Blocking diodes prevent reverse current at night. Use Schottky for low loss or ideal diode ICs for efficiency >99%.

4. USB-C Power Delivery

USB-C can deliver up to 100W (20V/5A). LTC4359 ideal diode controller provides <20mV drop at full load. Critical for laptop power inputs.

5. Industrial 24V Equipment

Field wiring errors common. Use P-FET with 15V Zener gate clamp. Consider fuse + FET combination for overcurrent and reverse protection.

6. LED Drivers

LEDs are diodes - reverse polarity won't conduct but may damage driver. Series Schottky simple solution. For high-power strings, use FET-based protection.

7. PoE (Power over Ethernet)

Polarity may vary by pair usage (Mode A vs B). Full-bridge rectifier handles either polarity but drops 1V. Alternative: dual P-FET configuration.

8. E-Bike/EV Battery Packs

48V/72V packs with 20-50A discharge. Back-to-back FETs for bi-directional protection. Monitor gate voltage for safe operation.

9. Audio Equipment (Guitar Pedals)

9V barrel jacks have no polarity standard. Series diode acceptable for low-current (<100mA) pedals. Boss-style center-negative is common but not universal.

10. Medical Device Battery Backup

Critical applications need ideal diode OR-ing for seamless failover. LTC4412/LTC4416 provide <20mV drop with load-sharing capability.
