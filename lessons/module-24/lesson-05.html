Oscillation Causes and Fixes

Symptom Frequency Cause Fix
Continuous sine Near bandwidth Insufficient phase margin Add compensation (Cc)
Ringing on edges MHz range Underdamped LC/RLC Add series R, snubber
Motor-boating Hz to kHz Power supply coupling Bypass caps, decoupling
VHF parasitic >100MHz Lead inductance Ferrite beads, layout
Squegging Irregular bursts Gain margin cycling Reduce gain, add damping
Output swinging rail-to-rail Low frequency Feedback polarity wrong Swap FB connection
