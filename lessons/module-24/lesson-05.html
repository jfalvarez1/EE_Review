Industrial 4-20mA Current Loop Design

The 4-20mA current loop has been the backbone of industrial instrumentation for decades. Its noise immunity over long distances and ability to detect faults make it ideal for process control applications.

4-20mA Loop Design Checklist

Progressive Exercises

Level 1: Fundamentals

Exercise 1: A 4-20mA signal represents 0-100 PSI. What pressure does 12mA represent?

12mA is 50% of 4-20mA range (8mA of 16mA span)

Exercise 2: A receiver uses a 250Ω sense resistor. What voltage corresponds to 20mA?

V = I × R = 20mA × 250Ω

Level 2: Application

Exercise 3: A 2-wire transmitter operates from 24V loop with 100Ω cable + 250Ω receiver. What voltage is available to the transmitter at 4mA?

V_drop = 4mA × (100+250)Ω. V_avail = 24 - V_drop

Exercise 4: A transmitter needs 3.5mW to operate. At 4mA with 10V headroom, is it within budget?

P_avail = V × I = 10V × 4mA = 40mW. Is 3.5mW < 40mW?

Exercise 5: A 12-bit ADC with 5V reference samples a 250Ω current loop. What is the current resolution in µA per LSB?

V_lsb = 5V / 4096. I_lsb = V_lsb / 250Ω × 1e6
