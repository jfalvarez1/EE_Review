Wireless Charging (Qi) Design

Wireless power transfer enables convenient charging without cables. This lesson covers inductive coupling principles, Qi protocol, and circuit design for transmitters (TX) and receivers (RX).

Industry Reference: Based on TI Wireless Charging Design Guide, Analog Devices Wireless Charging Solutions, and Wireless Power Consortium (WPC) Qi specifications.

Section 3: Foreign Object Detection (FOD)

FOD prevents heating of metallic objects (coins, keys) on the charging surface:

FOD Methods

Method Implementation Accuracy
Power Loss Compare TX input power vs RX reported power Good (standard Qi)
Q-Factor Monitoring Measure coil Q change during ping High
Capacitive Sensing Detect object capacitance on surface Medium
Thermal Sensors Monitor surface temperature Backup safety
Safety Critical: FOD is essential for safety. Metallic objects can heat rapidly (10-50W absorbed) and cause burns or fire. Qi certification requires robust FOD with <500mW threshold.
