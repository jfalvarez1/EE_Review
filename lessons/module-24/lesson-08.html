Common EMI Symptoms and Sources

Symptom Likely Source Frequency Debug Approach
Noise synced to switching SMPS, PWM motor kHz-MHz + harmonics Scope sync to switcher, filter
Noise when MCU active Digital clock harmonics Clock freq × N Spread spectrum, shielding
RF pickup on audio AM/FM/cell broadcast 500kHz - 2GHz RF bypass caps, ferrites
ESD causing resets Human body discharge Broadband ns pulse TVS diodes, filtering
Fails near motors/relays Inductive switching Broadband spikes Snubbers, isolation
