Power Supply Debugging

Power supply problems cause more than half of all electronic failures. Master systematic approaches to diagnosing no-output, low-output, noisy, and unstable power supply issues.

No Output
Protection, open path, dead IC
Low Output
Overload, feedback, component
Noisy
Ripple, switching artifacts
Unstable
Oscillation, loop issues
Debug Principle: Start with DC measurements before connecting oscilloscope. Wrong DC levels indicate component failures; correct DC with noise issues points to loop or filtering problems.
