Solar MPPT Charge Controller Design

Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) extracts maximum energy from solar panels by continuously adjusting the operating point. This lesson covers MPPT algorithms and power stage design for solar charging systems.

Industry Reference: Based on TI Solar MPPT Design Guide, Analog Devices Solar Harvesting, and Victron SmartSolar reference.
