Thermal Problem Symptoms

Symptom Likely Cause Test Method Fix
Works when cold, fails when warm Parameter out of spec at temp Freeze spray isolation Replace failing part, derate
Drift over time after power-on Tempco mismatch Monitor vs junction temp Match tempcos, add compensation
Random shutdowns under load Thermal shutdown triggering IR camera, temp logging Improve cooling, reduce power
Burnt smell, discoloration Chronic overheating Visual inspection Redesign thermal path
Intermittent at certain temps Solder joint crack (CTE) Thermal cycling test Rework joint, stress relief
