Battery-Powered IoT Sensor

This project combines ultra-low-power design, energy harvesting, wireless communication, and precision sensing to create a battery-powered IoT sensor node that can operate for years on a single coin cell. Key challenges include managing sleep/wake cycles, minimizing quiescent currents, and optimizing radio transmission efficiency.

Battery:CR2032 (225mAh)
Sleep Current:<1 μA
Target Life:>5 years
Transmission:BLE/LoRa
Design Philosophy: Every microamp counts. The key to long battery life is aggressive power gating, efficient duty cycling, and careful selection of components with ultra-low quiescent currents. The radio typically dominates power consumption.
