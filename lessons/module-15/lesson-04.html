Charging Phases

Phase Condition Action Duration
Precharge Vcell < 3.0V Charge at C/10 Until Vcell > 3.0V
CC (Fast Charge) 3.0V < Vcell < 4.2V Constant current (1C typical) ~1 hour to 80%
CV (Saturation) Vcell = 4.2V Constant voltage, I tapers ~1 hour for remaining 20%
Termination I < C/10 Stop charging Cell is full
Maintenance After termination Optional trickle or recharge Continuous if enabled

Design Equations

Charge Current:
    Icc = C-rate × Capacity
    Example: 1C × 2000mAh = 2000mA = 2A

CC Phase Time (to ~80%):
    tcc ≈ 0.8 × Capacity / Icc
    Example: 0.8 × 2000mAh / 2000mA = 0.8 hours

CV Phase Time (for remaining ~20%):
    tcv ≈ 0.5 to 1 hour (depends on termination)

Total Charge Time:
    ttotal = tcc + tcv ≈ 1.5-2 hours at 1C

Temperature Compensation:
    T < 10°C: Reduce Icc to C/2, Vmax to 4.1V
    10-45°C: Normal charging
    T > 45°C: Stop charging (safety)

Voltage Accuracy Required:
    ±0.5% of 4.2V = ±21mV
    Use precision reference and feedback

Cycle Life vs Charge Voltage:
    4.20V: 100% capacity, 500 cycles
    4.10V: 85% capacity, 1000 cycles
    4.00V: 75% capacity, 2000 cycles
