Cell Balancing Circuits

Multi-cell battery packs require balancing to prevent overcharge of individual cells and maximize usable capacity. Learn passive and active balancing topologies and design trade-offs.

Passive
Bleed excess as heat
Active
Shuttle charge between cells
Top Balance
Balance when charging
Imbalance
Typically 10-50mV
Why Balance? Cells have slight capacity differences. During charge, weaker cells reach 4.2V first. Without balancing, charging must stop early, leaving stronger cells undercharged and reducing pack capacity.
