Microphone Preamps

Microphone signals are very low level (mV or less) and require high gain, low noise amplification. The preamp is often the most critical stage in the audio chain.

Dynamic Mic Level
-60 to -40 dBV (1-10mV)
Condenser Mic Level
-40 to -20 dBV (10-100mV)
Key Insight: At these low signal levels, the preamp's input-referred noise dominates. A 1nV/√Hz op-amp with 20kHz bandwidth has ~140nV total noise—significant compared to 1mV signals!

Phantom Power (48V)

Condenser microphones require DC power for their internal preamp:

Voltage
48V DC (±4V tolerance)
Current
2-10mA per mic
Feed Resistors
6.8kΩ matched pair
Balanced Input Required: Phantom power uses the balanced pair (pins 2 and 3) with equal DC on both. Ground (pin 1) carries return current. Dynamic mics ignore phantom if wiring is correct.
