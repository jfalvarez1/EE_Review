Audio Measurement and Test

Accurate measurement is essential for designing and verifying audio equipment. Understanding THD, noise floor, frequency response, and other metrics allows engineers to characterize performance and identify problems.

Key Measurements

  • THD+N: Total harmonic distortion + noise
  • SNR: Signal-to-noise ratio
  • Frequency response: Gain vs frequency
  • Crosstalk: Channel separation

Test Equipment

  • Audio analyzer: AP, dScope, etc.
  • FFT analyzer: Spectral analysis
  • Oscilloscope: Time-domain view
  • Multimeter: DC and RMS levels

A-Weighting Filter

A-weighting approximates human ear sensitivity, emphasizing frequencies where hearing is most sensitive (1-5 kHz) and attenuating low and high frequencies.
