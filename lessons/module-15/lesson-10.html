Reference Formulas

dBµV to V: V = 10(dBµV-120)/20

V to dBµV: dBµV = 20log(V) + 120

LC Filter: fc = 1/(2π√LC)

Attenuation (above fc): A = 40log(f/fc) dB (2nd order)

Shielding (plane wave): SE = 20log(1 + Z0/(2Zs)) dB

Loop Antenna E-field: E = 1.3×10-14 × f² × A × I / d (V/m)
