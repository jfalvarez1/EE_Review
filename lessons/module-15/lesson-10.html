Volume Control and VCA Circuits

Volume control ranges from simple potentiometers to sophisticated voltage-controlled amplifiers (VCAs). Understanding the logarithmic nature of human hearing is essential for designing controls that feel natural to users.

Human Hearing

  • Dynamic range: ~120 dB (threshold to pain)
  • Perception: Logarithmic (Weber-Fechner law)
  • 1 dB: Smallest perceptible change
  • 10 dB: Perceived as "twice as loud"

Volume Control Types

  • Potentiometer: Passive, simple, audio taper
  • Stepped attenuator: Precision, repeatable
  • VCA: Voltage-controlled, remote control
  • Digital: PGA, resistor ladder DAC

Audio Taper Potentiometer

Audio (logarithmic) taper pots provide perceived linear volume change as the knob rotates.

Voltage-Controlled Amplifier (VCA)

VCAs allow electronic volume control, essential for automation, remote control, and dynamic processing.
