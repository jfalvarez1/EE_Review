Reference Formulas

Temperature Rise: ΔT = P × θ

Junction Temp: Tj = Ta + P × (θJC + θCS + θSA)

Max Power: Pmax = (Tj,max - Ta) / θJA

Series θ: θtotal = θ1 + θ2 + θ3 ...

Parallel θ: 1/θtotal = 1/θ1 + 1/θ2 ...

TIM Resistance: θ = t / (k × A)
