Battery Chemistry Selection

Choosing the right battery chemistry is critical for product success. Each chemistry has distinct characteristics for energy density, power capability, cycle life, safety, cost, and operating temperature range. This lesson compares common chemistries and provides selection guidelines for different applications.

Chemistry TypesLi-ion, LiFePO4, NiMH, Lead-Acid, LiPo
Key MetricsWh/kg, Wh/L, C-rate, Cycles
Safety FactorThermal runaway, Overcharge tolerance
Cost Driver$/kWh varies 10x across chemistries
Design Philosophy: No single chemistry is best for all applications. Match the chemistry to your specific requirements: high energy for portables, high power for tools, long life for grid storage, low cost for backup power.

Li-ion Cathode Chemistry Details

Within Li-ion, different cathode materials offer different tradeoffs:

LCO (LiCoO2): High energy density (200+ Wh/kg), limited cycle life (~500), expensive cobalt, used in phones/laptops. Avoid high-C applications.
NMC (LiNiMnCoO2): Balanced performance, good energy (150-220 Wh/kg), moderate cycle life (1000+), mainstream chemistry for EVs and power tools.
NCA (LiNiCoAlO2): Highest energy (250+ Wh/kg), used by Tesla, requires careful thermal management, sensitive to overcharge.
LFP (LiFePO4): Excellent safety and cycle life (2000-5000), lower energy density (90-160 Wh/kg), no cobalt, flat discharge curve.
LTO (Li4Ti5O12): Titanate anode, exceptional cycle life (10000+), very safe, can charge at high C-rates, but lower voltage (2.4V) and energy density.

Temperature Performance

Battery performance varies significantly with temperature. Select operating range carefully.

Li-ion Operating Range-20°C to +60°C (discharge), 0°C to +45°C (charge)
LiFePO4 Range-20°C to +60°C (better cold performance)
Lead-Acid Range-20°C to +50°C (capacity drops 50% at -20°C)
NiMH Range0°C to +45°C (poor cold performance)
