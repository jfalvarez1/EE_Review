Algorithm Comparison

Method Accuracy Drift Complexity Best Use
Voltage (OCV) ±10-15% None Low Standby, light loads

Impedance Track ±1-3% Self-correcting High Aging batteries
Extended Kalman ±1-2% Minimal Very High Critical applications
ML/Neural Net ±1% Minimal Extreme Research, EVs
