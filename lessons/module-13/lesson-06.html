CAN Bus Physical Layer

Controller Area Network (CAN) is a robust differential bus designed for automotive and industrial applications. Its dominant/recessive architecture enables collision-free arbitration and fault-tolerant communication.

Key Insight: CAN uses "dominant" (logic 0) and "recessive" (logic 1) states. Any node can drive the bus dominant, overriding recessive states. This wired-AND behavior enables non-destructive arbitration—the lowest ID message wins without collision.

Bus Length vs Speed: Higher speeds require shorter buses. At 1 Mbps, max length is ~40m. At 125 kbps, up to 500m is possible. The limiting factor is propagation delay relative to bit time.
