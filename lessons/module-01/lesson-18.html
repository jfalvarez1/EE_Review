BJT Active Loads

Replacing passive resistors with transistor current sources (active loads) dramatically increases amplifier gain. This technique is fundamental to op-amp design and any high-gain analog circuit.

Resistor Load Gain
Av = -gm × RC
Active Load Gain
Av = -gm × (ro1 || ro2)
Industry Use: Op-amp gain stages, OTAs, current-mode circuits, ADC/DAC current sources, precision references.

Why Active Loads?

Load Type Impedance Gain (IC = 1mA) Limitation
Resistor (10kΩ) 10kΩ -400 (gm=40mS) DC drop, area on IC
Simple Mirror ro ≈ 50kΩ -2000 Early effect
Cascode Mirror gmro² ≈ 2MΩ -80,000 Headroom

With active loads, the output impedance is limited by the transistor's ro, not a physical resistor value.

Simple Current Mirror as Active Load

Circuit Analysis

  • Q2-Q3 form a PNP current mirror
  • Q3 provides constant current to Q1
  • Output at Q1 collector has impedance ro1 || ro3
  • Gain = gm1 × (ro1 || ro3)
