Fully Differential Amplifiers

Fully differential amplifiers (FDAs) have differential inputs AND differential outputs. They are essential for driving high-performance ADCs, providing superior noise immunity, and doubling the signal swing in low-voltage systems.

Single-Ended vs Differential

Key FDA Concepts

Output Common-Mode Voltage (VOCM)

VOCM = (VOUT+ + VOUT−) / 2

Differential Output Voltage

VOD = VOUT+ − VOUT−

FDA Gain Configuration

Single-Ended to Differential Conversion

A common use case is converting a single-ended signal to differential for driving an ADC.

Key Point

The VOCM pin is typically connected to the ADC's common-mode reference output. This ensures the FDA's output is centered at the ADC's optimal input range, usually mid-supply.

Advantages of Fully Differential

  • Double the signal swing: VOD = 2 × single-ended swing for same supply
  • Common-mode noise rejection: Noise coupling equally to both outputs cancels
  • Even harmonic cancellation: Push-pull symmetry reduces HD2
  • Optimal ADC interface: Matches differential ADC inputs directly
  • Reduced EMI: Equal and opposite currents reduce radiated emissions
