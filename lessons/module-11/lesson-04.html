System-Level Trade-offs

Optimizing individual blocks doesn't guarantee optimal system performance. Understanding how blocks interact and where to allocate performance budget is crucial.

Noise Budget
Total = √(e₁² + e₂² + ... + eₙ²)
Error Budget
RSS of independent errors
Key Insight: RSS (Root Sum Square) addition means the largest contributor dominates. Reducing a 1% error to 0.1% matters little if another stage has 3% error. Fix the worst first!

Where to Add Gain

Early Gain
Reduces impact of later stage noise
But Watch Out
Early gain also amplifies sensor noise!
Optimal Gain Distribution: Place gain where SNR is worst. If sensor has 10× more noise than amp, adding gain before amp wastes power. Match stage noise to signal level.
