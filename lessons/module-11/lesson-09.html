Resolution vs Speed Trade-offs

In data conversion and measurement systems, resolution (number of bits) and speed (sample rate or conversion time) are fundamentally at odds. Understanding this trade-off helps select the right converter architecture for your application.

Resolution Factors

  • Quantization noise: Decreases 6dB per bit
  • DNL/INL: Harder to achieve at high resolution
  • Noise floor: Must be below LSB
  • Comparator precision: µV-level decisions

Speed Factors

  • Settling time: Must complete each step
  • Comparator delay: Decision time per bit
  • Reference settling: DAC output stabilization
  • Aperture jitter: Limits effective resolution
