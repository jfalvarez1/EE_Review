Jitter and Phase Noise

Jitter is the time-domain manifestation of phase noise, which is the frequency-domain representation of timing uncertainty. Understanding both is essential for designing high-speed digital and RF systems.

Time Domain: Jitter

  • Period jitter: Cycle-to-cycle variation
  • Cycle-cycle jitter: Adjacent period diff
  • TIE: Time interval error
  • Units: ps, fs RMS or pk-pk

Frequency Domain: Phase Noise

  • L(f): SSB phase noise (dBc/Hz)
  • Offset: Distance from carrier
  • Integrated: RMS jitter from integral
  • Spurs: Discrete tones

Jitter Sources and Budget

Key Insight: Random jitter sources add in RMS (root-sum-square), while deterministic jitter adds directly (peak-peak). This is why identifying and minimizing the dominant jitter source is crucial.
