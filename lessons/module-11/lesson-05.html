Noise vs Bandwidth Trade-offs

Total integrated noise increases with bandwidth, creating a fundamental tension between signal speed and noise performance. Understanding this trade-off is essential for optimizing signal-to-noise ratio in any analog design.

Key Relationships

  • Thermal noise: Vn = √(4kTRB)
  • Shot noise: In = √(2qIB)
  • 1/f corner: Transition to pink noise
  • Noise BW: Often ≈ 1.57 × f-3dB

Design Strategies

  • Limit bandwidth: Only what's needed
  • Averaging: Trade time for SNR
  • Oversampling: Spread noise, filter
  • Chopping: Move 1/f noise up
