Schmitt Triggers & Hysteresis

Schmitt triggers use positive feedback to create hysteresis—different thresholds for rising and falling edges. This noise immunity is essential for clean digital signals from noisy analog inputs.

Key Insight: Once the input crosses a threshold, the output snaps to the opposite state. The input must travel back past the other threshold to switch again. This prevents noise from causing multiple transitions.

Design Rule: Hysteresis should be 2-3× the expected noise amplitude for reliable operation. Too little hysteresis defeats the purpose; too much wastes signal range.
