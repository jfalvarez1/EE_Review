Reliability vs Cost Trade-offs

Product reliability directly impacts warranty costs, customer satisfaction, and brand reputation. However, designing for higher reliability typically increases component costs, testing expenses, and design complexity. Understanding these trade-offs helps engineers make informed decisions for their target market.

Reliability Factors

  • Component quality: Grade, screening
  • Derating: Operating below limits
  • Redundancy: Backup systems
  • Testing: Burn-in, stress screening

Cost Factors

  • Component cost: 2-10× for hi-rel
  • Testing time: Burn-in adds lead time
  • Design margin: Oversizing components
  • Documentation: Traceability requirements
