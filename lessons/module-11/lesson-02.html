Power vs Performance Trade-offs

Power consumption affects noise, speed, and thermal performance. Battery-powered designs require careful optimization while maintaining required specifications.

Slew Rate
SR = Ibias / Ccomp
Noise Density
en ∝ 1/√(Ibias)
Key Insight: For a given process, doubling the bias current doubles the slew rate but only reduces noise by √2 (1.4×). Power is the universal currency in analog design.

Technique Savings Trade-off

Shutdown/sleep modes 90%+ when idle Wake-up time, complexity
Duty cycling Proportional to duty Settling time on wake

Figure of Merit

Op-Amp FoM
FoM = GBW × CL / P
ADC FoM
FoM = P / (2ENOB × fs)
Process Limits: Each process node has fundamental limits on power efficiency. State-of-the-art designs push these boundaries but can't exceed them.
