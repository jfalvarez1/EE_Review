Clock Distribution Networks

Distributing a clean clock signal across a PCB or system while maintaining signal integrity is critical for reliable digital operation. This lesson covers clock buffer selection, tree topologies, and skew management.

Key Challenges

  • Skew: Arrival time differences
  • Jitter accumulation: Each stage adds
  • Signal integrity: Reflections, crosstalk
  • EMI: Clock is prime radiator

Design Goals

  • Matched delays: Equal path lengths
  • Low additive jitter: Clean buffers
  • Proper termination: Match Z0
  • Controlled edges: Balance SI/EMI
