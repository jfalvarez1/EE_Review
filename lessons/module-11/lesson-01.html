LC Oscillators

LC oscillators use inductors and capacitors to generate sinusoidal signals. The Barkhausen criterion requires loop gain = 1 and phase shift = 0° (or 360°) for sustained oscillation.

Resonant Frequency
f = 1 / (2π√LC)
Barkhausen Criterion
|Aβ| ≥ 1, ∠Aβ = 0°
Key Insight: The LC tank circuit provides frequency selectivity. At resonance, the tank presents maximum impedance, and energy oscillates between the inductor's magnetic field and capacitor's electric field.

Colpitts vs Hartley

Topology Feedback Network Advantages
Colpitts Capacitor divider (C1, C2) Better frequency stability, simpler
Hartley Inductor tap (L1, L2) Easier gain adjustment
Clapp Series L-C with cap divider Best frequency stability

Topology Schematics

Colpitts: capacitor divider feedback
Hartley: tapped inductor feedback
Clapp: Colpitts + series capacitor for stability

Design Considerations

Tank Q
Higher Q = purer sine, slower startup
Amplitude Limiting
Transistor saturation or AGC
Frequency Pulling
Load affects frequency slightly
Practical Issues: Component tolerances affect frequency accuracy. Temperature changes L and C values. Use NP0/C0G capacitors and air-core inductors for stability.
