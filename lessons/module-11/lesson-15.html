Spread Spectrum Clocking

Spread spectrum clocking (SSC) reduces electromagnetic interference by modulating the clock frequency, spreading the energy across a wider bandwidth. This lesson covers SSC techniques, modulation profiles, and EMI compliance considerations.

SSC Benefits

  • EMI reduction: 10-20 dB typical
  • Compliance: FCC, CE testing
  • No shielding: Reduced cost
  • Harmonics: Spreads all harmonics

SSC Parameters

  • Spread: ±0.25% to ±2.5%
  • Modulation rate: 30-100 kHz
  • Profile: Triangle, Hershey Kiss
  • Direction: Center, down, up
