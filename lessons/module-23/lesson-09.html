Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) Design

PoE delivers power alongside data over standard Ethernet cables, enabling IP cameras, VoIP phones, and IoT devices without separate power supplies. This lesson covers PSE (Power Sourcing Equipment) and PD (Powered Device) design.

Industry Reference: Based on Analog Devices PoE Solutions, TI PoE Design Guide, and IEEE 802.3af/at/bt standards.

PoE Standards Overview

Standard Name Max Power (PD) Voltage Range Use Case
IEEE 802.3af PoE (Type 1) 12.95W 36-57V VoIP phones, basic cameras
