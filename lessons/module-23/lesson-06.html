Case Study: Medical ECG Amplifier

Design a patient-safe ECG front-end amplifier with proper isolation, low noise, and high CMRR. This scenario covers the unique challenges of medical instrumentation including safety standards and biopotential measurement.

Safety First: Patient safety requires galvanic isolation between patient and earth ground. Leakage current must be <10µA under single fault condition. The right-leg drive actively cancels common-mode interference.

The Scenario

Requirement: Design 3-lead ECG front-end for portable monitor.

  • Input: Two differential electrodes + right-leg reference
  • Gain: 1000× total (instrumentation amp + programmable)
  • Noise: <2µVpp referred to input (0.05-150Hz)
  • CMRR: >100dB at 50/60Hz
  • Defibrillator protection required
  • Battery powered, isolated
