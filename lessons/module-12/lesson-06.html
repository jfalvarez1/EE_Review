Schmitt Trigger Inputs

Schmitt trigger inputs provide hysteresis that makes digital interfaces immune to noise and slow-transitioning signals. They're essential for interfacing with mechanical switches, optocouplers, and any signal that doesn't have clean edges.

Standard vs Schmitt Trigger Input

CharacteristicStandard InputSchmitt Trigger
ThresholdSingle (VIH/VIL)Two (VT+/VT-)
HysteresisNoneVT+ - VT- (typ 0.2-1V)
Slow edgesMay oscillateClean transition
Noise immunityLowHigh
Current (CMOS)Near-zero staticNear-zero static
