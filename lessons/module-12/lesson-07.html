Bus Buffers and Drivers

Bus buffers and drivers increase the current drive capability, voltage translation, and fan-out of digital signals. They're essential for driving long traces, multiple loads, or crossing voltage domains in parallel buses.

When to Use Bus Buffers

SituationProblemBuffer Solution
High fan-outMCU can't drive >4 loadsBuffer provides 24mA+ drive
Long tracesSlow edges, reflectionsLow-impedance driver
Voltage translation3.3V MCU to 5V peripheralsDual-supply buffer
IsolationProtect MCU from shortsBuffer absorbs faults
Impedance matchingTransmission line effectsControlled slew rate
