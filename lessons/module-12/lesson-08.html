Signal Termination

At high speeds or with long traces, digital signals behave as transmission lines. Proper termination prevents reflections that cause signal integrity problems like ringing, overshoot, and false triggering.

When Termination is Needed

The rule of thumb: if the signal rise time is less than twice the propagation delay of the trace, termination is necessary.

If trise < 2 × tprop, then terminate
