Push-Pull vs Open-Drain Outputs

Digital outputs come in two fundamental configurations: push-pull (totem-pole) and open-drain (open-collector for BJT). Understanding when to use each is essential for proper interface design.

Output Stage Comparison

CharacteristicPush-PullOpen-Drain
Active HIGH driveYes (low impedance)No (requires pull-up)
Active LOW driveYes (low impedance)Yes (low impedance)
Wired-OR/AND possibleNo (contention!)Yes
Different voltage levelsNo (fixed to VCC)Yes (via pull-up voltage)
Rise timeFast (driven)Slow (RC limited)
Power consumptionLow (static)Higher (pull-up current when LOW)
