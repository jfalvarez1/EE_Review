Gate Drivers and Bootstrap Circuits

Power MOSFETs and IGBTs require significant gate charge to switch quickly. Gate drivers provide the current capability and voltage levels needed. For high-side N-channel MOSFETs, bootstrap circuits generate the above-rail gate voltage required. Understanding these circuits is essential for power electronics design.

Gate Driver Requirements

  • Peak current: 1-10A typical
  • Rise/fall time: 10-50ns
  • Gate charge: 10-1000nC
  • Under-voltage lockout: Prevents weak drive

Bootstrap Benefits

  • Single supply: No isolated supply needed
  • Low cost: Capacitor + diode
  • Compact: Minimal components
  • Widely used: Standard in motor drives
