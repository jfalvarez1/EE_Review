Class A Output Stages

Class A amplifiers bias the output transistor to conduct continuously throughout the entire signal cycle. This eliminates crossover distortion but wastes significant power as heat.

Max Efficiency
ηmax = 25% (resistive load)
Quiescent Power
PQ = Vcc × Ibias
Key Insight: In Class A, the transistor always conducts at least Ibias. Even with no signal, it dissipates V×I continuously. Maximum output occurs when transistor swings between 2×Ibias and 0, but average current stays at Ibias.

Thermal Considerations

Max Dissipation
At zero signal! (Not max output)
Heatsink Sizing
RθJA < (Tj,max - Tamb) / Pdiss
Thermal Runaway: BJT current increases with temperature. Without thermal feedback (emitter resistor), the transistor can overheat and fail. Always include thermal stabilization.
