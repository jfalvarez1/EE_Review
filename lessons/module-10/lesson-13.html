Environmental Testing and Reliability

Products must operate reliably across their intended environmental conditions. Temperature, humidity, vibration, and ESD exposure accelerate failure mechanisms. Understanding these tests helps engineers design robust products and predict field reliability.

Goal
Verify design works across all conditions
Key Metric
Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)

Operating Temperature Ratings

Grade Temperature Range Application
Commercial 0°C to +70°C Consumer electronics, office equipment
Industrial -40°C to +85°C Factory automation, outdoor equipment
Automotive -40°C to +125°C Under-hood, engine compartment
Military -55°C to +125°C Defense, aerospace
Component Derating: Operating at maximum rated temperature reduces lifetime. Industrial-grade components in commercial applications provide margin for reliability.

Common Environmental Tests

Test Standard Purpose
Thermal cycling IPC-9701, JESD22-A104 Solder joint fatigue from CTE mismatch
Thermal shock MIL-STD-883, JESD22-A106 Rapid temperature change tolerance
Temperature/humidity JESD22-A101 (85/85) Moisture ingress, corrosion
HALT (Highly Accelerated Life Test) Internal specification Find design margins and weaknesses
Vibration MIL-STD-810, IEC 60068-2-6 Mechanical stress, fatigue
Drop/shock IEC 60068-2-27 Impact resistance
ESD immunity IEC 61000-4-2 Electrostatic discharge protection

Thermal Cycling

Parameter Typical Value
Temperature range -40°C to +125°C (automotive) or -55°C to +125°C
Ramp rate 10-15°C/minute
Dwell time 10-15 minutes at each extreme
Number of cycles 500-1000 for qualification, 1000+ for reliability
CTE Mismatch: Different materials expand at different rates. PCB (FR4) ≈ 14-17 ppm/°C, silicon ≈ 3 ppm/°C. This mismatch stresses solder joints during cycling.

85/85 Testing (Temperature/Humidity)

Conditions
85°C, 85% relative humidity
Duration
1000 hours typical

Failure Mechanisms Accelerated:

  • Electrochemical migration (dendrite growth between traces)
  • Corrosion of metals
  • Moisture absorption in plastics/packages
  • Delamination of PCB layers
  • Flux residue activation
No-Clean Flux: While convenient, no-clean flux residue can become conductive under 85/85 conditions. Consider washing for high-reliability applications.

ESD Protection Levels

Model Level Typical Test
HBM (Human Body Model) ±2kV to ±8kV 100pF through 1.5kΩ
CDM (Charged Device Model) ±500V to ±2kV Fast transient to device pin
IEC 61000-4-2 (System level) ±8kV contact, ±15kV air Discharge gun to enclosure

Reliability Prediction

Arrhenius Equation: AF = exp[Ea/k × (1/Tuse - 1/Ttest)]

Where: AF = Acceleration Factor, Ea = activation energy (0.7eV typical), k = Boltzmann constant
Test Temp Use Temp Acceleration Factor
125°C 55°C ~60×
125°C 40°C ~175×
85°C 40°C ~10×
1000 Hours at 125°C: Roughly equivalent to 10+ years at 40°C for many failure mechanisms. This is why high-temperature life tests are common.

HALT/HASS Testing

HALT
Highly Accelerated Life Test (design phase)
HASS
Highly Accelerated Stress Screen (production)

HALT Process:

  1. Cold step stress: Find lower operating limit
  2. Hot step stress: Find upper operating limit
  3. Rapid thermal transitions
  4. Vibration step stress: Find vibration limit
  5. Combined stress: Temperature + vibration simultaneously

Goal: Push product beyond specification limits to find design margins and weak points before production. Fix issues found during HALT to improve field reliability.
