Thermal Design for Output Stages

Output stages often dissipate significant power, making thermal design critical. This lesson covers heatsink selection, thermal interface materials, and complete thermal stack analysis for reliable operation.

Thermal Goals

  • Tj < Tj(max): Usually 150-175°C
  • Safe margin: Keep Tj < 125°C
  • Reliability: Lower temp = longer life
  • Derating: Account for worst case

Thermal Resistances

  • θjc: Junction to case
  • θcs: Case to heatsink (TIM)
  • θsa: Heatsink to ambient
  • θja: Total junction to ambient

Thermal Interface Materials

Material Thermal Conductivity Typical θcs Notes
Thermal paste (silicone) 0.7-3 W/m·K 0.2-0.5 °C/W Lowest cost, messy
Thermal paste (metal oxide) 3-8 W/m·K 0.1-0.3 °C/W Better performance
Thermal pad (silicone) 1-6 W/m·K 0.5-1.5 °C/W Easy handling
Phase change material 0.7-3 W/m·K 0.1-0.3 °C/W Reflows at temp
Insulating pad (Sil-Pad) 0.9-3 W/m·K 0.8-2 °C/W Electrical isolation
