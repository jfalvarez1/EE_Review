Class AB Biasing Techniques

Class AB amplifiers bias the output transistors to conduct slightly even at idle, eliminating crossover distortion while maintaining high efficiency. The bias circuit must track temperature to prevent thermal runaway. This lesson covers the most common biasing approaches.

Biasing Goals

  • Eliminate crossover: Small Iq (10-50mA typical)
  • Track temperature: VBE drops ~2mV/°C
  • Low source impedance: Drive bases quickly
  • Stability: Prevent thermal runaway

Common Approaches

  • Diode biasing: 2 diodes in series
  • VBE multiplier: Adjustable, tracks temp
  • Current source bias: Constant Iq
  • Servo bias: Feedback-controlled
