Efficiency vs Linearity Trade-offs

In amplifier design, power efficiency and signal linearity are often opposing goals. High efficiency typically requires switching or operating transistors near saturation/cutoff, while linearity demands operation in the linear region with significant headroom. Understanding this trade-off is critical for RF, audio, and power amplifier design.

Efficiency Metrics

  • Drain efficiency: η = Pout/Pdc
  • PAE: (Pout - Pin)/Pdc
  • Conduction angle: Time device is on
  • Back-off: Operating below max power

Linearity Metrics

  • THD: Total harmonic distortion
  • IMD: Intermodulation distortion
  • 1dB compression: Onset of saturation
  • IP3: Third-order intercept point
