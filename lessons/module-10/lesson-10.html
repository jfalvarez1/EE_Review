Cascode and Darlington Configurations

Cascoding stacks transistors to improve output impedance and bandwidth, while Darlington pairs multiply current gain. Both techniques are fundamental building blocks in output stages, current sources, and amplifier designs.

Cascode Benefits

  • Output impedance: Increased by β
  • Bandwidth: Reduces Miller effect
  • Voltage capability: Shares voltage
  • Isolation: Shields input from output

Darlington Benefits

  • Current gain: β1 × β2
  • High input impedance: β² × re
  • Simple drive: Low base current
  • Available as single device: TIP120
