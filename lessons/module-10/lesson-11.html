EMI vs Performance Trade-offs

Electromagnetic interference (EMI) mitigation often conflicts with circuit performance goals. Faster edges improve efficiency but radiate more; filtering reduces noise but adds delay; shielding increases weight and cost. Engineers must balance compliance requirements against system performance.

EMI Sources

  • Fast edges: dV/dt and dI/dt
  • Switching frequency: Harmonics
  • Ground bounce: Return path inductance
  • Coupling: Capacitive and inductive

Mitigation Impact

  • Slower edges: Lower efficiency, delay
  • Filtering: Cost, size, insertion loss
  • Shielding: Weight, cost, thermal
  • Spread spectrum: Timing jitter
