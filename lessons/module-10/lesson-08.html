Level Shifters for Output Stages

When control signals operate at logic levels (3.3V or 5V) but output stages run at higher voltages (12V, 24V, or higher), level shifting is required. This lesson covers bidirectional and unidirectional level shifting techniques for driving power stages.

Level Shifting Needs

  • Logic to gate: 3.3V → 12V gate drive
  • Isolation: Ground potential difference
  • Speed: Nanosecond transitions
  • Bidirectional: I2C, open-drain buses

Common Methods

  • MOSFET: BSS138 + pull-ups
  • Dedicated IC: SN74LVC1T45
  • Resistor divider: High-to-low only
  • Optocoupler: Galvanic isolation
