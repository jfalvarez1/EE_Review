Low-Current BJT Operation

Operating BJTs at microamp currents enables ultra-low-power designs but introduces challenges: β degradation, leakage dominance, and reduced fT. Understanding these limits is essential for battery-powered systems.

Low-Current Design Guidelines

Parameter Concern Mitigation
β degradation Current gain drops 2-10× at nA levels Use Darlington, accept lower β
Leakage currents ICBO becomes significant fraction of IC Guard rings, low-leakage selection
Matching VBE mismatch increases at low IC Larger area devices, careful layout
fT reduction Bandwidth falls linearly with IC Accept lower speed or parallel devices
Noise 1/f noise increases, optimal IC shifts down Larger devices, filtering
