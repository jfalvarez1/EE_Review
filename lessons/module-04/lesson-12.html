Design for Manufacturing (DFM)

A brilliant design that cannot be manufactured reliably is a failed design. Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles ensure your product can be produced consistently, at scale, and at acceptable cost. Understanding manufacturing constraints early prevents costly respins.

DFM Goal
Maximize yield, minimize rework and cost
Key Principle
Design with manufacturing tolerances in mind

Yield Impact: Pushing to minimum capabilities reduces yield. Use 6/6 mil (trace/space) unless absolutely necessary—it's cheaper and more reliable than 4/4.

Solder Paste and Stencil Design

Stencil Thickness
4-5 mil typical, 3 mil for fine-pitch
Area Ratio
>0.66 for reliable paste release
Area Ratio: AR = Aperture Area / Wall Area = (L × W) / (2 × (L + W) × T)

Where T = stencil thickness. AR > 0.66 ensures paste releases from aperture.

Panelization

Feature Purpose Guidelines
Panel rails Machine handling 5-10mm on 2+ sides, fiducials on rail
V-score Board separation Straight lines only, avoid near components
Tab routing Complex board shapes 3+ tabs per board, mouse bites for easy break
Bad mark area Reject tracking Space for X mark on failed boards
V-Score Stress: Components within 2mm of V-score lines may experience stress during depanelization. Keep sensitive components away from break lines.
