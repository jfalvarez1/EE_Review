Case Study 1: Wideband Amplifier

Requirement: 20V output swing, 5ns rise time, 50Ω load

Design Step Calculation Result
Bandwidth needed BW = 0.35/tr 70 MHz
Peak current Ipeak = V/RL 400 mA
Transistor fT fT > 10 × BW > 700 MHz
Topology Cascode for bandwidth CE-CB cascade
Key Insight: Cascode eliminates Miller effect. Choose a high-fT transistor like 2N5109 (fT = 1.2GHz) or BFR92A.

Case Study 3: Low-Noise Audio Preamp

Requirement: 40dB gain, <1nV/√Hz input noise, 20Hz-20kHz

Design Step Approach Result
Transistor SSM2210 matched pair (low noise) 0.9nV/√Hz @ 1mA
Collector current Optimize for noise vs. bandwidth 1-2mA per transistor
Topology Differential pair + active load High CMRR, low noise
Source impedance Match RS for optimal noise ~600Ω microphone

Case Study 4: 5W Class AB Amplifier

Requirement: 5W into 8Ω, THD <0.1%, ±15V supplies

Parameter Calculation Value
Vpeak √(2 × P × RL) 8.9V
Ipeak Vpeak/RL 1.1A
Pdiss (each) VCC2/(π2 × RL) 2.8W
Bias (VBE multiplier) 2.4V drop for NPN+PNP R1/R2 ratio = 2.4
