Level Shifter Circuits

Level shifters translate signals between different voltage domains. Choosing the right topology depends on direction (unidirectional vs bidirectional), speed requirements, and whether it's push-pull or open-drain.

Level Shifting Topologies

TopologyDirectionSpeedBest Use Case
Resistor DividerHigh→Low onlyMediumSimple voltage reduction
N-FET + Pull-upBidirectionalLow-MediumI2C, open-drain buses
Dual-Supply BufferUnidirectionalHighSPI, high-speed signals
Auto-sensingBidirectionalMedium-HighGPIO, data buses
