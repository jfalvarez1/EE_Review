LVDS High-Speed Signaling

Low-Voltage Differential Signaling (LVDS) enables high-speed data transmission with low power and reduced EMI. Its 350mV differential swing and constant-current driver architecture make it ideal for multi-gigabit applications.

Data Rate
Up to 3.125 Gbps
Swing
±350mV differential
Common Mode
1.2V typical
Termination
100Ω at receiver
Key Insight: LVDS uses a constant-current driver (~3.5mA) that steers current through the differential pair. The receiver's 100Ω termination creates the 350mV voltage swing. This current-mode operation provides excellent noise immunity and minimal ground bounce.
