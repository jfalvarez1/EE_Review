Battery Protection Circuits

Battery protection prevents damage from overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent, and short circuits. Learn protection IC architectures and design considerations for safe battery operation.

Protection Features

Protection Trigger Action Recovery
Overcharge (OVP) Vcell > 4.25-4.35V Turn off charge FET Voltage drops below threshold
Over-discharge (UVP) Vcell < 2.5-3.0V Turn off discharge FET Charger connected
Overcurrent discharge I > limit (5-30A) Turn off discharge FET Load removed, delay
Overcurrent charge I > charge limit Turn off charge FET Current drops, delay
Short circuit I >> limit, fast Immediate FET off Load removed, long delay
Overtemperature T > 60-80°C Turn off both FETs Temperature drops

Design Equations

Current Sensing:
    Vsense = I × Rsense
    Rsense = Vth_ocp / Iocp

    Example: 100mV threshold, 5A limit
    Rsense = 0.1V / 5A = 20mΩ

Power Dissipation:
    Psense = I² × Rsense = 5² × 0.02 = 0.5W

MOSFET Selection:
    Vds_rating > Vpack × 1.5 (margin)
    Id_rating > Iocp × 1.5
    Rds_on: Lower = less loss, higher = self-limiting

    Power in FET: Pfet = I² × Rds_on

Protection Delay Times:
    OVP delay: 50-500ms (filter noise)
    UVP delay: 50-500ms
    OCP delay: 5-20ms
    SCP delay: <100µs (must be fast!)

Hysteresis:
    OVP: ~50-100mV below trip
    UVP: ~100-300mV above trip
    Prevents oscillation near threshold
