Optocouplers and Isolation

Optocouplers (optoisolators) provide galvanic isolation between circuits using light. They're essential for safety isolation in high-voltage systems, ground loop elimination, and noise-immune signal transfer.

Why Isolation is Needed

ApplicationIsolation ReasonTypical Voltage
Mains-powered equipmentSafety (human protection)2500-5000 Vrms
Industrial I/OGround loop elimination500-1500 Vrms
Medical devicesPatient safety4000-5000 Vrms
