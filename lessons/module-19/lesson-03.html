Common SI Problems

Symptom Likely Cause Solution
Ringing after edges Unterminated line, mismatch Add series/parallel termination
Overshoot > 30% Source impedance too low Add series resistor at source
Slow rise time RC loading, long stubs Reduce capacitive load, remove stubs
Glitches on quiet line Crosstalk from adjacent Add spacing, ground guard
Signal shifts with activity Ground bounce, SSO More power/ground pins, decoupling
Termination Trade-offs:
  • Series termination: Low power, works for point-to-point only
  • Parallel termination: Multi-drop okay, but constant current draw
  • AC termination (RC): Compromise, lower DC power
